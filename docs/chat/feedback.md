# Feedback

You can tell us when a reply was good or bad, and report a message. Both are optional. Neither changes the conversation.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > the icons under a reply: thumbs up, thumbs down, flag

## Rate a reply

1. Tap the thumbs up (**Good reply**) or thumbs down (**Bad reply**) under a reply.
2. Pick any reasons that fit. All are optional.
3. Tap **Send**.

Reasons for a thumbs up: **In character**, **Listened to me**, **Fresh idea**, **Remembered something**, **Right length**, **Hot**, **Something else**.

Reasons for a thumbs down: **Repeats herself** (or **himself**), **Ignored what I said**, **Out of character**, **Made something up**, **Sent me away**, **Too long**, **Too short**, **Crossed a limit**, **Something broke**, **Something else**.

- You can rate a reply once. The thumb stays visible afterwards.
- Without the log, a rating is only counted. It is not linked to you or to the message. [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md)

## Report a message

1. Tap the flag (**Report this message**) under a reply.
2. Choose a reason: **Inappropriate content**, **Technical issue**, **Wrong information** or **Other**.
3. Add details if you like.
4. Tap **Submit Report**.

You see **Reported. Thank you.** when it is sent.

## Also send my log

Both sheets have a box **Also send my log for this message**.

- It is **never ticked for you**. You tick it, per message, if you want to.
- If you tick it, we receive the conversation up to that message, what the chat tells your Domina about you (profile, limits, items, goal, routines) and the app's technical log. It is stored encrypted for up to 90 days so we can look into what you reported or rated.
- Your safe word is never included.
- To delete logs you sent, go to **You** > **Data Privacy** > **Logs you sent**. The row appears once you have sent one.
- If the log could not be sent, the rating or report still went through.

The box only shows when logs can be received. If you do not see it, nothing is sent.

## Send us a report file

When something went wrong and you want us to look at it, you can save a file with what the app did recently.

1. Tap the bug icon (**Debug**) at the top of the chat, next to the **Chat menu**.
2. The dialog **Send us a report** opens.
3. Tap **Download** to save the file, **Clear** to empty what the app has collected so far, or **Cancel**.
4. Send the file to us through **You** > **Help & support** (Discord or email).

- The file contains your conversation. Treat it like a chat log and send it only to us.
- Your safe word and your sign-in details are removed from the file.
- Nothing is sent by the button itself. The file stays on your device until you send it.

## Trace

**Chat menu** > **Settings** > **Trace** shows, under each reply, a read only view of what ran behind it: the reply itself, what your Domina wrote to memory, and which cards came up.

- It is off at first. Switch it on if you are curious or want to describe a problem to us.
- It only shows. It never changes what your Domina does.
- It is a setting on this device.

## Good to know

- Thumbs and flags appear under your Domina's replies only, not under yours.
- A report is not an emergency line. If something is too much right now, use the safe word. [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md), [Not okay?](../safety/not-okay.md)
