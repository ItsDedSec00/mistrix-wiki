# Timers

When your Domina gives you something to do for a set time, a small countdown chip appears at the top right of the chat. It keeps the time so you do not have to.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat**, top right, under the header

## How it works

- The chip shows the time. While it runs it also shows the name of the hold.
- It does not start by itself. Tap it where it says **Tap to start**.
- Tap it again while it runs to dismiss it.
- When time is up it rings, and vibrates where your device supports it. It rings a few more times until you dismiss it. The chip then reads **Done. Tap to dismiss**.
- When the time runs out, your Domina is told and carries on. You do not have to type that it is over.
- Your Domina can set a timer when something is meant to last a set time. If no chip appears, ask for one and say how long.

## Good to know

- The chip is kept on the device you use. If you leave the chat and come back during the same visit, it is still there. A chip set on your phone does not appear on your laptop.
- A chip from an earlier visit, long over, is not brought back.
- If you regenerate the reply that set the timer, or delete it with **Delete this and everything after**, the chip goes with it. [Edit & Regenerate](edit-and-regenerate.md)
- Sound needs your device volume on and the app open. A locked phone may delay the bell until you return.
- A timer is not a safety device. Your safe word always works. [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md)
