# Chat

The chat is where everything happens. You write, your Domina or Dom answers in text. These pages explain the screen.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** tab, or **Dashboard** > **Continue conversation**

The **Chat menu** ([glossary](../help/glossary.md#chat-menu)) is the button at the top right of the chat. Its panel is headed **Overview** and has the tabs **Personality**, **Memory**, **Journal**, **Fetishes**, **Gallery** and **Settings**. The **Journal** tab only shows when it is available for your chat.

## Pages

- [Dominas](choosing-a-domina.md): open a chat with a preset or your own Domina.
- [Messages](messages.md): writing, Continue, the warning line under a reply, "In a scene" and "Just talking".
- [Photos](photos.md): sending a photo, the pictures your Domina offers, what they cost.
- [Timers](timers.md): the countdown chip for timed holds.
- [Cards](cards.md): the boxes that appear in the conversation, and what each button does.
- [Edit & Regenerate](edit-and-regenerate.md): change a message, ask for a new reply, delete from a point.
- [Feedback](feedback.md): thumbs, reasons, reporting a message, and sending your log.

## Always within reach

- The **Safe word** button is in the **Chat menu** on every tab. Typing your safe word on its own also stops everything. [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md)
- Every feature can be switched off by you. [Turning things off](../safety/turning-things-off.md)
- If something is too much: [Not okay?](../safety/not-okay.md)
