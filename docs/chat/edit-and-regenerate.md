# Edit & Regenerate

Under every message there are small icons. They let you fix a message, ask for a different reply, or step back in the conversation.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > the icons under a message. They appear when no reply is being written and no demand is open.

## What each icon does

| Icon | On | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Pencil (**Edit**) | your message | Opens the text. **Send** replaces your message **and everything after it** with a new turn. |
| Pencil (**Edit**) | their reply | Changes the reply in place. **Save** keeps your version. Nothing is sent again. |
| Circular arrow (**Regenerate**) | their reply | Asks for a new reply to the same message. |
| Rewind (**Delete this and everything after**) | your message | Deletes that message and everything after it. Nothing is sent. |

## How it works

- Editing a photo message edits its caption. The photo stays.
- Press **Escape** to leave the editor, or **Cancel**.
- On the Free plan, **Edit** on your message uses the message allowance like a new message, and **Regenerate** is not offered while the allowance is used up. [Free plan](../plans/free.md)
- Regenerate is offered only on replies that follow one of your messages, so not on the opening message of a chat.
- The messages are kept encrypted with your PIN. Deleting is final: there is no undo. [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md)

## Good to know

- These actions change the conversation only. A payment you made, a picture you bought, or a lock change that already went through is not undone.
- The warning line under a reply goes away when you edit that reply. [Messages](messages.md)
- A timer your Domina set goes with the reply when you regenerate it or delete it. [Timers](timers.md)
- Messages far back in a long history cannot be edited or deleted. The icons do not show on them.
- To erase the whole conversation, use **Reset chat & memories** in the **Chat menu**. It also erases what your Domina remembers. [How she remembers](../memory/index.md)
