# Dominas

Each Domina or Dom has their own chat. You can open one from the **Chat** tab, from the Dashboard, or from the **Dominas** screen.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** tab, **Dashboard** > **Continue conversation**, or **Dominas** > tap a card

## How it works

- The **Chat** tab and **Continue conversation** open your main Domina. On the Free plan that is the preset you picked. On Premium and Premium Plus it is your own active Domina if you built one, otherwise the preset you talked to last.
- To talk to someone else, open **Dominas** and tap a card. The cards are sorted under **Dominas**, **Doms** and **Trans**. The **Kinks** menu narrows the list.
- Tap the **i** on a card to read about them before you open the chat. Locked cards have no **i**.
- Every Domina keeps **their own conversation and their own memory of you**. Switching does not mix them up.
- Your safe word, your Charms, and your feature settings (Findom, Chastity, Expose) belong to your account. They are the same in every chat.
- Inside a chat, tap the round picture in the header to see your Domina full-screen. Some presets show a short intro clip under their first message.
- The line under the name shows when your Domina is **pleased with you** or **displeased with you**. When neither applies, the line stays empty. [Mood](../dominas/mood.md)

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    Free accounts can open the free presets. The cards marked **Upgrade to unlock** and your own Dominas need Premium or Premium Plus. A personal Domina you built stays saved if you drop to Free, and returns as she was when you upgrade. [Plans](../plans/index.md)

## Steps

1. Open **Dominas**.
2. Pick **Dominas**, **Doms** or **Trans** at the top.
3. Tap a card to open the chat.

## Good to know

- On the Free plan you chat with one preset at a time. Tapping a different preset asks you to confirm the switch first. Read the dialog before you confirm.
- Resetting a chat erases the conversation and what that Domina remembers about you. It is in the **Chat menu** as **Reset chat & memories**. [How she remembers](../memory/index.md)
