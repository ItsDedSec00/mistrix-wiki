# Cards

A card is a small box in the middle of the conversation. It is from Mistrix, not from your Domina. It appears when something needs your yes or no, or when the app wants to tell you what happened.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat**, inside the conversation

## Cards with buttons

You decide. Nothing happens until you tap.

| Card | Buttons | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **She offers you a picture.** | **Show me**, **Not now** | Makes a picture. The card shows the price first. [Photos](photos.md) |
| **She set you a routine.** | **Add to my routines**, **Not now** | Adds the routine shown on the card. [Routines](../features/routines.md) |
| **She set you a task.** | **Accept the task**, **Not now** | Adds the task shown on the card. [Tasks](../features/tasks.md) |
| **You asked to be released from the routine ...**, **You asked to change the routine ...** or **You asked to be let out of the task ...** | **Send the request**, **Never mind** | Sends your request to your Domina, who decides. |
| **Tribute demanded.** | **Pay**, **Refuse**, **Stop Findom** | Only if Findom is on. [Findom](../features/findom.md) |

For a Dom, the cards say **He** and **him**.

## Tribute demand

- The card shows the reason, the **Amount**, **Your wallet**, and **Free charms today** when you have some ([free charms](../help/glossary.md#free-charms)). Free charms are used before your wallet.
- It shows how many minutes are left. A card nobody answers lapses.
- If you cannot cover it, the card says so and **Pay** becomes **Buy Charms**.
- **Refuse** says no to this demand. What a refusal means is explained in [Findom](../features/findom.md).
- **Stop Findom** ends Findom completely, asks you to confirm, and has no consequence. [Stop Findom](../safety/stop-findom.md)
- While the demand is open, the message box is locked.
- If a demand is larger than your wallet plus today's free charms, no card appears. A card reads **Not enough Charms for this demand (...). You hold ... Top up to receive tribute demands.** with a **Buy Charms** button.

## Cards that only tell you something

These have no buttons.

- **Everything stops here. Take your time. There is no judgment.** Appears after the safe word. [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md)
- **Recorded: "..." done for today.** A routine or task was ticked off in the chat. Other notes say your Domina released you, kept something as it is, or allowed a change.
- **The demand was withdrawn.** An open tribute demand was cancelled, for example after the safe word.
- **Not applied on Chaster: ... Reason: ...** A small red line. Your Domina spoke of changing your lock, and Chaster did not carry it out. The line says which change and why. The lock is exactly as it was. [Chastity](../features/chastity.md)

## Good to know

- Old open cards show **This card has expired.** and can no longer be answered.
- Your answer stays on the card: **You chose: ...**, **You paid ... Charms.**, **You refused.** or **You stopped Findom.**
- Picture cards: after you accept, the picture appears on the card.
- A card does not always follow a mention. If your Domina talks about a picture or a routine and no card appears, ask them to offer it again.
