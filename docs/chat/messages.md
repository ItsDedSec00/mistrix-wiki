# Messages

You write in the box at the bottom. Your Domina or Dom answers in text, one reply per message. Everything in the chat is AI-generated, and a line under the box says so.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > the box at the bottom. The indicator is in **Chat menu** at the top.

## How it works

- **Send:** tap **Send**. On a keyboard, **Enter** starts a new line. **Shift+Enter**, **Ctrl+Enter** or **Cmd+Enter** sends.
- **Continue:** when the box is empty, **Send** turns into an arrow (**Continue**). It asks your Domina to carry the moment on without you typing. Your side stays empty.
- **While they write:** three dots appear, then the words arrive one by one. The line under their name reads *typing...*. If you prefer the finished message in one piece, switch off **Stream her reply** (**Stream his reply** for a Dom) in **Chat menu** > **Settings**.
- **Voice input:** where your browser supports it, a microphone button appears when the box is empty. Tap it, speak, and your words are typed for you. Hold it to listen for "Mistress" (or "Sir" for a Dom) followed by your message.
- **Add to chat:** the **+** button next to the box sends a photo or pins a note. A pinned note (up to 500 characters) stays in view of your Domina on every message and stays on this device. [Photos](photos.md)
- **Routines and tasks:** the checklist button next to it opens your routines and tasks. [Routines](../features/routines.md) and [Tasks](../features/tasks.md)
- **Older messages:** scroll to the top and tap **Show earlier messages** or **Load older messages**.
- **Time:** what your Domina knows about the time is your choice. **Chat menu** > **Settings** > **What she knows about the time** offers **Real time**, **Time between** and **No time**.

## In a scene or just talking

Above the tabs in the **Chat menu**, a small line tells you what kind of moment the chat is in:

- **In a scene.** Your Domina is leading play right now.
- **Just talking.** No scene is running. Your Domina talks with you and sets nothing unless you ask.

The line updates after each reply. It describes the chat, it is not a switch.

## The warning line

Sometimes a short line with a warning sign appears under a reply, outside the message. It is from Mistrix, not from your Domina. Examples:

- Never restrict breathing or circulation.
- Use clamps with caution, max 15 minutes.
- Expose your information with caution.
- Mistrix should not control your eating or body weight.
- Meeting a stranger carries real risk.
- If you feel unwell, stop and check now.

The reply is not changed and the chat is not blocked. Take the line seriously and stop if something feels wrong. [Not okay?](../safety/not-okay.md)

## Good to know

- Your safe word typed on its own, as the whole message or as its own sentence or phrase, works like the button. [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md)
- Your Domina can be wrong, repeat themselves, or forget. Use [Feedback](feedback.md) when a reply is off.
- On the Free plan, messages use a refilling allowance (the [message meter](../help/glossary.md#message-meter)). When it is used up, the box says so and a countdown appears. [Free plan](../plans/free.md)
- While a Findom demand is open, the box is locked until you pay, refuse or stop it. [Cards](cards.md)
- If your newest messages cannot be saved yet, a red bar tells you. Keep the app open and do not clear the browser.
