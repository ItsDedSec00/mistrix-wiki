# Photos

You can send your Domina or Dom a photo, and they can offer you a picture of themselves. Both appear in the chat and are kept in the **Gallery** tab of the **Chat menu**.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Sending: **Chat** > **+** > **Take a photo** or **Choose from gallery**
    Your pictures: **Chat menu** > **Gallery**

## Sending a photo

1. Tap the **+** button next to the message box.
2. Choose **Take a photo** or **Choose from gallery**.
3. Add a few words if you like. They are optional.
4. Tap **Send to her** (**Send to him** for a Dom).

- Your Domina reads your words and the photo together and answers to what they see.
- A photo with no words shows only the photo.
- A photo counts as a message.

!!! warning "Photos can fail to be read"
    If the photo could not be read, a line under it says so: **This photo could not be read.** and that your Domina has not seen it. Anything they say about it then comes from your words alone. Send it again, or describe it.

If **Take a photo** and **Choose from gallery** are missing under **+**, photos are not available right now.

If the upload fails, a short message appears in the chat: your Domina could not look at the photo right now, the image could not be sent, or the photo could not be sent. Try again or choose another photo.

Photos you send are saved with that chat. How they are stored is explained once in [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).

## Pictures from your Domina

- Now and then your Domina offers you a picture. A card appears with **Show me** and **Not now**. [Cards](cards.md)
- The card shows what it costs before you accept: either **free pictures left today**, or the Charm price and your balance. If you cannot afford it, the button reads **Buy Charms**.
- After you accept, the picture is made. A placeholder reading **Making her picture...** (or **his**) appears, then the picture. Tap it to enlarge.
- If a picture fails, you are refunded and the card says so.
- Do not want offers? Switch off **Offer me pictures** in **Chat menu** > **Settings**. Your Domina stops bringing it up.

The free pictures come from your free Charms for the day, which are shared with AI Studio. [Charms](../plans/charms.md)

## The Gallery tab

- Your photos are marked with a camera, pictures from your Domina with a star.
- Tap a picture to enlarge it.
- **Show it to her again** (**him** for a Dom) puts a picture back in front of your Domina so they can react to it.
- **Delete picture** removes it for good from the gallery.
