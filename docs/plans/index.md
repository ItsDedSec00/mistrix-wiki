# Plans

Mistrix has three plans and one in-app currency. This tab covers what each plan gives you, what costs Charms, and how you move up.

## Pages

- [Free](free.md): what you have from the start, and how the message meter works.
- [Premium](premium.md): your own Domina, every preset, higher limits.
- [Premium Plus](premium-plus.md): up to three Dominas of your own and the highest limits.
- [Charms](charms.md): your wallet, the free charms you get each day, and what costs Charms.
- [Shop](shop.md): buy Charms, change your plan, cancel.
- [Referrals](referrals.md): invite friends and both of you earn.
- [XP & Badges](xp-and-badges.md): experience levels 0 to 100 and the leaderboard choice.

## Plans side by side

| | Free | Premium | Premium Plus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Presets | The ones marked free | All | All |
| Your own Dominas | None | 1 | Up to 3 |
| Chat messages | A meter that refills through the day | No meter | No meter |
| Free charms each day | Yes | More | Most |
| Active routines | 4 | 10 | 20 |
| Routines your Domina confirms | No | Yes | Yes |
| Routine history | Current week | Last 4 weeks | Last 4 weeks |
| Open tasks at a time | 1 | No cap | No cap |
| Inventory | 5 items per category | 30 items | 30 items |
| **Context** setting | Up to 60,000 characters | Up to 120,000 | Up to 120,000 |
| **Enhance** when saving in AI Studio | No | Yes | Yes |
| Let your Domina freeze a lock timer | No | Yes | Yes |
| **High** notification frequency | No | Yes | Yes |

!!! info "Where the current values are"
    The Shop shows the free charms per day for the plans you can still upgrade to. The plans screen shows the current price. The limits in the table are the ones the app itself shows when you reach them.
