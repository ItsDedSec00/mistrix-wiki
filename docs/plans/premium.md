# Premium

Premium is the monthly subscription for your own Domina, every preset and higher limits.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Plan** > **Subscription**. The **Shop** also has an **Upgrade your tier** section.

## What you get

- One Domina you build yourself. See [Your own Domina](../dominas/your-own-domina.md).
- Every preset, including the ones marked Premium. See [Presets](../dominas/presets.md).
- No message meter in the chat.
- More free charms each day than on Free. See [Charms](charms.md).
- Routines your Domina can confirm for you, up to 10 active, and your routine history for the last four weeks. See [Routines](../features/routines.md).
- No cap on open tasks. See [Tasks](../features/tasks.md).
- Up to 30 inventory items. See [Inventory](../memory/inventory.md).
- A larger **Context** setting, up to 120,000 characters. See [Context](../memory/context.md).
- Pictures your Domina sends in the chat arrive at a higher resolution. See [Cards](../chat/cards.md).
- **Enhance** when you save a picture in AI Studio, at no extra cost. See [AI Studio](../features/ai-studio.md).
- The permission to let your Domina freeze your lock timer. See [Chastity](../features/chastity.md).
- The **High** notification frequency. See [Notifications](../features/notifications.md).

## Steps

1. Open **You** > **Plan** > **Subscription**.
2. Choose **Premium** on the plans screen and tap **Unlock Premium**.
3. Pay in the checkout. The final amount is shown there.

The plans screen shows the current price.

## Good to know

- You cancel in the billing portal, under **You** > **Plan** > **Subscription**. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.
- In the last days of a cancelled plan, the **Dashboard** shows how many days are left and a button to keep your plan.
- If you leave, your own Domina is kept and shown as resting. It comes back as it was when you subscribe again.
- Refunds follow the Terms. See [Shop](shop.md).
