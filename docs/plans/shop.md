# Shop

The Shop is where you buy Charms, change your plan and find your invite link.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Plan** > **Buy charms**. Any card that says you need more Charms also leads here.

## How it works

The Shop has three parts, top to bottom:

- **Upgrade your tier**: Premium and Premium Plus, each with a short list of perks and the free charms per day. It disappears once you are on Premium Plus. See [Premium](premium.md) and [Premium Plus](premium-plus.md).
- **Invite & Earn**: your invite link and progress. See [Referrals](referrals.md).
- **Charm bundles**: one-time purchases.

Your balance sits at the top as **Wallet**.

## Charm bundles

| Bundle | Charms |
|---|---|
| Starter pouch | 50 |
| Velvet purse | 150 |
| Black coffer | 500 |

- Each bundle shows its price and the price per charm. Prices come live from the payment service Stripe, and the final amount is confirmed at checkout.
- A bundle can carry a **Popular** or **Best value** badge.
- A bundle marked **Unavailable** cannot be bought right now.

## Steps

1. Tap **Buy** on a bundle.
2. Pay in the checkout. It has a field **Add promotion code** if you have one.
3. You return to a confirmation screen. It reads **Processing...** first, then **Payment received**. Tap **Buy more** to go back to the Shop. The Charms appear in your **Wallet**.

## Change or cancel your plan

- Free to a paid plan: **Upgrade to Premium** or **Upgrade to Premium Plus** opens the checkout.
- Between paid plans, and to cancel: the billing portal under **You** > **Plan** > **Subscription**. It shows the change before anything is charged.
- Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.
- While a cancellation is scheduled, a card on the **Dashboard** lets you keep your plan. It may offer a discount on your next payment.

## Refunds

- Charms are not refunded once spent. A picture or clip that fails is refunded automatically.
- Tribute is paid in Charms and is not refunded.
- For subscriptions, the Terms say the right of withdrawal for digital content ends once the service has started with your consent, so elapsed subscription periods are not refunded. Exceptions apply where mandatory consumer law says otherwise.
- Questions about a payment: **You** > **Help & support**.

## Good to know

- If a purchase does not show up, wait a moment on the confirmation screen, then reopen the Shop. See [Fix a problem](../help/fix-a-problem.md).
- Charms expire if your account is deleted.
