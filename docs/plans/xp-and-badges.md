# XP & Badges

XP shows how much you use Mistrix. You earn it by chatting and finishing things, and it moves you up a ladder of levels with a badge and a rank name.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    The card at the top of **You** shows your badge, rank, level, XP and the way to the next level. The **Dashboard** shows your level and rank too. The leaderboard choice is under **You** > **Data Privacy**.

## How it works

- Everyone starts at Level 0, the Stranger.
- There are 100 levels. Each one needs more XP than the one before.
- A notice tells you when you reach a new level.
- Your level does not change how demanding your Domina is. That is the **Severity** setting. See [Severity](../dominas/severity.md).

## What earns XP

- Chatting. A small amount comes as you go.
- A routine done for the day, whether you tick it or your Domina confirms it. See [Routines](../features/routines.md).
- A task done. See [Tasks](../features/tasks.md).
- A picture you accept in the chat. See [Cards](../chat/cards.md).
- A [tribute](../help/glossary.md#tribute) you pay, if you use Findom. See [Findom](../features/findom.md).
- A post your Domina makes on your Expose board. See [Expose](../features/expose.md).

Each of these counts once. If you undo a routine or task, its XP is taken back. XP from each source is limited per day, so it adds up over days and not in one sitting.

## Badges and ranks

You carry the rank of the last badge you reached.

| Level | Rank | Badge |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Stranger | Locked ring with a 0 |
| 1 | Applicant | White ring |
| 5 | Pet | White ring, a little thicker |
| 10 | Plaything | White ring, thicker |
| 15 | Servant | White ring, thicker |
| 20 | Devoted | White ring, thickest |
| 30 | Collared | Bronze |
| 40 | Owned | Silver |
| 50 | Property | Gold |
| 100 | Favourite | Animated colour spectrum |

The card on **You** shows how much XP is left to the next level and, when it is a different step, the next badge and its level.

## Leaderboard choice

- **Show my username on leaderboards** is a switch under **You** > **Data Privacy**. You also choose it when you sign up.
- On: your username appears next to your place on the leaderboards, including the one on the Mistrix Discord server.
- Off: your place, level, rank and XP still appear, but as **Anonymous**, without your username.
- You can change it at any time. A change reaches the leaderboard after a short while.

What is stored and shared is described on [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
