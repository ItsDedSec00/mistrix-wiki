# Free

Free is the plan you start on. You can chat, use AI Studio, keep routines and fill in your profile without paying.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu**. The **Free plan** box sits at the top, above the tabs.

## How it works

- You can open every Domina marked free. Premium Dominas show with a lock on the Dominas screen. See [Presets](../dominas/presets.md).
- A Domina you build yourself needs Premium. See [Your own Domina](../dominas/your-own-domina.md).
- Your messages run on a meter. The **Free plan** box shows how many you have left and a clock for the next one.
- The meter refills through the day, one message at a time. You do not have to wait for it to be full.
- Sending, editing and continuing a reply use a message.
- Your Domina writing to you first never costs a message. See [Contact](../dominas/contact.md).
- You get free charms every day for AI Studio pictures and tribute. See [Charms](charms.md).

## When your messages run out

- The chat shows a card, **Free messages used up**, with a countdown to the next message.
- You cannot send until the clock runs out or you pick an option on the card.
- Your safe word and the reply to it never use up messages. See [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md).
- A countdown timer running out in the chat does not use up a message. A regenerated reply does not either, but it needs at least one message left to start.

## Get more messages

The card and the **Free plan** box offer two ways, both paid in Charms from your wallet:

- **+N messages**: a button that adds a set number of messages on top of the meter.
- **Day Pass**: unlimited messages until midnight.

Each asks you to confirm before any Charms are spent. Free daily charms do not pay for these, only Charms you bought or earned. The buttons show the current price.

Or move to [Premium](premium.md), which has no message meter.

## Good to know

- Routines on Free are self-check: you tick them off. See [Routines](../features/routines.md).
- Free allows one open task at a time. See [Tasks](../features/tasks.md).
- Your inventory holds up to five items per category. See [Inventory](../memory/inventory.md).
- If you leave Premium for Free, a Domina you built is kept and rests. It is not deleted.
