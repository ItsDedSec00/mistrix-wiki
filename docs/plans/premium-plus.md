# Premium Plus

Premium Plus is the top plan. It has everything in Premium, with room for more Dominas and the highest limits.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Plan** > **Buy charms** opens the **Shop**. **Upgrade your tier** is at the top of it until you are on Premium Plus.

## What you get

- Everything in [Premium](premium.md).
- Up to three Dominas of your own. The Dominas screen shows how many you have. See [Your own Domina](../dominas/your-own-domina.md).
- Up to 20 active routines. See [Routines](../features/routines.md).
- The most free charms each day. See [Charms](charms.md).

## Steps

1. Open the **Shop**.
2. Under **Upgrade your tier**, tap **Upgrade to Premium Plus**.
3. From Free you go to the checkout. From Premium you go to the billing portal, which shows the change and asks you to confirm before anything is charged.

## Good to know

- You cancel the same way as Premium, in the billing portal under **You** > **Plan** > **Subscription**.
- The Shop shows the free charms per day for the plans you can upgrade to.
