# Charms

Charms are the in-app currency. You spend them inside Mistrix on pictures, videos, tribute and extra messages. Charms have no value outside the app.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Your balance shows as **Wallet** at the top of the **Shop**, as a number in the header of **AI Studio**, and under **You** > **Plan** > **Buy charms**.

## Two sources

- **Free charms**: a daily amount that comes with your plan. It refills once a day and you cannot save it up. The Shop shows the amount for each plan, and **AI Studio** shows how many are left today.
- **Wallet**: Charms you bought, earned through [Referrals](referrals.md), or got back as a refund.

## What costs Charms

- **Pictures in the chat**: the picture card shows the cost and your balance before you accept.
- **Images and clips in AI Studio**: the cost shows before you generate. Saving a preview and **Enhance** cost nothing extra. See [AI Studio](../features/ai-studio.md).
- **Tribute**: only if you switched Findom on. Every demand shows the amount before you decide. See [Findom](../features/findom.md).
- **Extra messages and the Day Pass**: only on the Free plan. See [Free](free.md).

## Which Charms pay first

- **Pictures and clips**: if the full cost fits in what is left of today's free charms, it is free. If not, the full cost comes from your wallet. The app does not split one picture between the two.
- **Tribute**: today's free charms count first, then your wallet pays the rest. The tribute card shows both.
- **Extra messages and the Day Pass**: wallet only. Free daily charms do not count, and the confirmation says so.

## When you run short

- A card or button that needs more than you have leads to the [Shop](shop.md).
- A Domina never demands more tribute than your wallet and free charms together hold. If a demand would be higher, you see a note that you need more Charms instead. See [Stop Findom](../safety/stop-findom.md) to end tribute at any time.

## Good to know

- If a picture or clip fails, the Charms for it are refunded and the card says so.
- Charms are not refunded once spent.
- Charms belong to your account. They expire if the account is deleted. See [Delete account](../help/delete-account.md).
