# Referrals

Share your personal link. When a friend signs up with it and starts using Mistrix, you both get Charms.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Plan** > **Buy charms** > **Invite & Earn**

## How it works

- **Invite & Earn** shows your link. **Copy link** copies it, and **Reddit** and **X** open a ready post.
- Your friend has to register through your link. A friend who signs up without it does not count.
- A friend counts once they have finished setting up and started chatting. Rewards arrive after a waiting period, not the moment they sign up. The Shop shows invites still waiting as **pending**.
- For each invite that counts, you and your friend each get Charms. The line above the bar shows the current amount. Your friend still gets theirs once you have reached the cap.
- The bar shows how many invites you have this quarter and the cap. At the cap you unlock a discount on Premium.
- Your link and your progress reset every quarter. The Shop shows the days left.

## Premium discounts

- **At the cap**: a button such as **Claim your 20% off** appears (the percent follows the current offer). On a paid plan it applies to your next payment. On Free it is saved and applies when you subscribe.
- **If a friend you invited goes Premium**: both of you get a discount on the next payment. The line shows **Ready** when it is waiting and **Claimed** once used. It can be used once per quarter.
- The second discount needs you to be on Premium. On Free the Shop shows **Unlock with Premium**.

## Good to know

- Referrals from the same person do not count.
- The Shop shows the current Charms and discount. They can change.
- Charms from referrals go into your wallet. See [Charms](charms.md).
