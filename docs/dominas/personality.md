# Personality

The **Personality** tab shows who your Domina or Dom is and lets you tell them how to sound and where they are.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Personality**

## How it works

From the top of the tab:

- **Mood**: how they feel about you right now. See [Mood](mood.md).
- **Who she is** (or **Who he is**), **Traits**, **Style** and their focus: a read-only description. Style shows style, tone, speech and **Intensity**.
- **What you want from her** (or him), with three fields you can edit:
    - **Key Memories**: see [Key Memories](../memory/key-memories.md).
    - **Response Directive**: see [Response Directive](../memory/response-directive.md).
    - **Example Message**: a message the way you want them to sound. They match its register, length and shape, and do not repeat it.
- **Where she is right now** (or **Where he is right now**), with **Scene setting**: where they are while you talk. They write as if the conversation happens there. Example: a flat, late evening, a glass of wine on the counter.

## Steps

1. Open the **Chat menu** and tap **Personality**.
2. Type into **Example Message** or **Scene setting**.
3. Stop typing. The field saves itself. There is no save button.

## Good to know

- Changes take effect from your next message.
- Each field shows a counter with its limit.
- The settings are per Domina. Another Domina keeps their own.
- The description and traits are not editable here. On a preset they are fixed. For your own Domina, change them with **Configure** on the Dominas screen: see [Your own Domina](your-own-domina.md).
- **Key Memories** is soft direction. **Response Directive** is meant to be followed in every reply. Like anything written by an AI, a reply can still miss them in a long chat. Your hard limits and safe word rank higher.
- What they remember about you is on the **Memory** tab: see [How she remembers](../memory/index.md).
- See [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md) for how your text is stored.
