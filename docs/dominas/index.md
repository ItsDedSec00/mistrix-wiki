# Dominas

Every Domina or Dom has their own chat, memories, mood and settings. These pages cover who they are and how to shape them.

- [Presets](presets.md): the ready-made Dominas, Doms and Trans Dominas, and which plan opens which.
- [Your own Domina](your-own-domina.md): build a character from scratch on Premium or Premium Plus.
- [Personality](personality.md): who they are, plus your Example Message and Scene setting.
- [Severity](severity.md): how hard their demands on you are, from 1 to 5.
- [Mood](mood.md): how they currently feel about you.
- [Contact](contact.md): whether they may write first and whether their notifications reach you.

Key Memories and Response Directive have their own pages: [Key Memories](../memory/key-memories.md) and [Response Directive](../memory/response-directive.md).
