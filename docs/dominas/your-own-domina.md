# Your own Domina

You design a character from scratch: who they are, how they speak, what they are built around and how they look. Each one gets their own chat.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Dashboard** > **Dominas** > **Craft your own Domina** (or the plus button at the top)

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    Premium allows 1 own Domina. Premium Plus allows up to 3. The screen shows your count, for example **Your Dominas (1/3)**.

## Steps

1. Tap **Craft your own Domina**.
2. **Identity**: choose **Domina**, **Dom** or **Trans Domina**, give a name and pick a style.
3. **Dynamic**: choose the role they take, and set **Strictness** from loving to strict.
4. **Voice**: choose tone and speech style, and set **Intensity**.
5. **Traits**: pick up to 6 character traits.
6. **Specialties** (optional): pick the kinks they are built around and how central each one is. Your hard limits always win over a specialty.
7. **Source**: choose **Describe her** (or **Describe him**) or **Start from a photo**.
8. **Face & Skin**, **Hair** and **Body**: set the look.
9. **Preview**: add a visual aesthetic if you like, then save.
10. On the portrait screen, **Regenerate** makes another picture, **Continue** keeps it, **Edit Domina** goes back.

## Start from a photo

- Pick a photo of one adult. It is described, and the description fills in face, hair and body for you.
- You still walk through the next three steps and can change everything.
- The photo is described, not copied. The portrait is made from the description and only loosely resembles the photo. The photo itself is not stored.
- If a photo cannot be used or read, the app tells you. Pick another one or describe the character yourself.

## Change or remove

- The gear button (**Configure**) on a card opens a list of sections: Identity, Dynamic, Voice, Traits, Specialties, Appearance and Portrait. Change one and tap **Save changes**.
- Saving never changes the portrait. A new one is always your choice, and the app asks.
- The trash button (**Delete**) removes a Domina for good, with their chat and memories. The confirmation says it cannot be undone.
- On the active Domina (the one you opened last) the confirmation reads **Delete & Start Over** and also removes the images you shared with them. If they were your only one, you can pick a preset or build a new one.

## Good to know

- Tap a card to open that Domina's chat. The [Personality](personality.md) tab shows what you built.
- The first message in a new chat fits the role you picked.
- A portrait from the builder does not cost Charms.

!!! warning "If you move to Free"
    Your Domina is kept, not deleted. On the Dominas screen they show as **Resting while you are on Free**, with no chat, settings or delete. Upgrade and everything returns as it was.

At your limit, the plus button explains it. On Premium it offers Premium Plus.
