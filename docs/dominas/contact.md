# Contact

A Domina or Dom can write to you first: after a quiet stretch, at a time they promised, or when a routine is due. You decide this for each Domina.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Settings** > **She may write first** and **Notifications from her**

    For a Dom the labels read **He may write first** and **Notifications from him**.

## How it works

Two switches, per Domina:

- **She may write first**: allows messages you did not ask for. The message is waiting in the chat when you open it, with or without notifications. It applies to that Domina on every device.
- **Notifications from her**: decides whether their notifications reach you, meaning their reminders and the heads-up that they have written.

Things to know about those messages:

- How long a Domina waits after a quiet stretch follows **Frequency** under **You** > **App** > **Notifications**: **Low**, **Normal** or **High**. **High** is a Premium setting. **Frequency** and **Quiet hours** appear once notifications are on for this device.
- They do not write into a conversation you are in the middle of, and they do not stack up messages while you have not answered.
- Several Dominas each keep their own pace. Their notifications are spaced out, so two never arrive at the same moment.
- It is only a message. It does not start a payment demand, a lock change or a post.
- On the Free plan these messages do not use up your free messages.
- Tapping a notification opens the Domina who sent it.

## Steps

1. Open the Domina's **Chat menu** > **Settings** and set the two switches.
2. For notifications, turn them on for this device: **You** > **App** > **Notifications**. See [Notifications](../features/notifications.md).
3. Choose **Frequency**, and set **Quiet hours** if you want them. Notifications stay silent between those hours, in your time.

## Good to know

- Switching **She may write first** off withdraws a message that is waiting.
- You can have messages without notifications: leave notifications off on the device and the message simply waits in the chat.
- Reminders for [routines](../features/routines.md) and [tasks](../features/tasks.md) use the same notification setup.
- The **Notifications** row under **You** appears only on devices that support them.
