# Severity

Severity is your own setting for how demanding a Domina or Dom is with you. You set it per Domina, and you can change it at any time.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Settings** > **Severity**

## How it works

Five steps:

| Step | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gentle** | Light demands, nothing that stings. For a first week or a tired evening. |
| 2 | **Measured** | Real demands with room to breathe. |
| 3 | **Firm** | Firm demands and firm follow-through. The middle of the scale. |
| 4 | **Hard** | Hard demands, little mercy, your limits as the only brake. |
| 5 | **Relentless** | Everything you can take, and the safe word is yours to use. |

- It changes how hard they are with you, not who they are. A gentle character at 5 is still that character.
- Changes apply from your next message.
- It is stored with that Domina, so it follows you to any device.
- Resetting the chat does not change it.

## Steps

1. Open the **Chat menu**.
2. Open the **Settings** tab.
3. Tap a step under **Severity**. The info mark next to the label explains the step you picked.

## Good to know

- A new chat starts at **Firm** at most, based on what you told Mistrix about your experience. Raise it if you want more.
- Your [hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md) and your [safe word](../safety/safe-word.md) apply at every step. Severity never overrides them.
- Do not confuse it with [Intensity](../help/glossary.md#intensity) and **Strictness** from [Your own Domina](your-own-domina.md). Those describe how the character was built. Severity is yours and is about you.
- The number guides how they behave. It is not an exact rule, and a long chat can drift.
- If it gets too much, use the [safe word](../safety/safe-word.md) or turn the setting down.
