# Mood

Each Domina or Dom has a mood toward you: **Pleased**, **Neutral** or **Displeased**. It colors how they write to you.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Personality** > **Mood**

## How it works

- **Neutral** is the starting point. A pleased Domina or Dom shows it in their own way. A displeased one holds back warmth and praise until you earn it back.
- When the mood is not neutral, a short reason appears under it.
- The mood moves with how the conversation goes. Being good to them can warm it, being sharp can cool it.
- If you refuse a [tribute](../features/findom.md) demand, the mood drops.
- It drifts back toward neutral over time.
- Each Domina or Dom has their own mood toward you.

## Reset it

- The [safe word](../safety/safe-word.md) sets the mood back to neutral.
- **Stop Findom** leaves the mood alone. It is not a refusal, and there is no punishment: see [Stop Findom](../safety/stop-findom.md).
- **Reset chat & memories** (bottom of the chat menu) clears the mood along with the chat.

## Good to know

- It is a mood, not a score you carry from one Domina to the next.
- Mood never changes your limits. Your hard limits always apply.
- The mood can lag or read oddly in a short chat. It tends to build up over several messages.
