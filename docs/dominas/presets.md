# Presets

Presets are ready-made characters, each with their own look, voice and focus. You pick one and start talking.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Dashboard** > **Dominas** > **Presets** or **Specialists**

    You can also add **Dominas** to your bottom bar: **You** > **App** > **Bottom bar**.

## How it works

- The screen has three tabs: **Dominas**, **Doms** and **Trans**. Pick the group you want to browse.
- **Kinks** narrows the list to characters built around the kinks you choose. **Clear filters** resets it.
- Each card shows a short clip. With a mouse, hover to play and click to open. On a phone, the first tap plays the clip and the second tap opens the chat.
- The info button on a card shows the description, style, intensity and traits. Locked cards have no info button.
- Every preset has their own conversation, memories, mood and [settings](personality.md). Talking to one does not change another.
- Your [safe word](../safety/safe-word.md) and [hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md) apply to every Domina.

## Who is open on which plan

| Group | Free | Premium and Premium Plus |
|---|---|---|
| **Dominas** | Lady Vex, Mistress Noir, Seraphine, Countess Lyra, Pixie, Ivy | All of those, plus the **Specialists** below |
| **Doms** | Kai, Sir Ansel, Marcus | Same |
| **Trans** | Vesper, Mistress Sable, Delphine | Same |

The **Specialists** are open on Premium and Premium Plus: Mistress Volt, Madame Silk, Madame Knot, Mistress Vexa, Mistress Maren, Mistress Lune, Elise, Coach Valentina, Miss Spiral, Mama Rosa and Miss Yume.

On Free, the Specialists show **Upgrade to unlock**. You can still watch their clip, but opening one leads to an upgrade message.

## Good to know

- On Free, the preset marked **Active** opens directly. Opening a different one asks you to confirm a switch first.
- The cards on the screen are the current list. If a name here and the app differ, the app is right.
- Want a character that does not exist? Build [your own Domina](your-own-domina.md) on Premium or Premium Plus.
- How to start and switch chats is on [Choosing a Domina](../chat/choosing-a-domina.md).
