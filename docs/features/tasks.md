# Tasks

A task is a single thing to do, once. Your Domina can set one in the chat, and you can add your own.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Routines** > **Tasks** (the first block on the screen).

## How it works

- A task your Domina offers in the chat arrives as a card. Tap **Accept the task** to keep it, or **Not now**. See [Cards](../chat/cards.md).
- Each task shows its deadline: **due today**, **due tomorrow**, **due in N days**, **overdue** (in red) or **no deadline**.
- A self-check task has a circle in front of it. Tap it, or open the task and tap **Mark done**.
- A task that says **report in chat** is confirmed by your Domina. Open it and tap **Tell (name) you did it**.
- Like a routine, a task has an owner label: **All Dominas** or one Domina's name. Tap it to change it.

## Steps

1. Open **Routines** and tap **Add** next to **Tasks**.
2. Enter a **Title** and an **Instruction**.
3. Optional: choose a **Deadline**: **No deadline**, **Tonight**, **Tomorrow**, **In 3 days** or **This week**.
4. Under **Completion**, choose **I mark it done** or **She confirms it**.
5. Tap **Create task**.

## Drop a task

- Tap **Drop** next to a task and confirm **Ask her**. This opens the chat and tells your Domina you want out of that task (for a task shared with **All Dominas** you first pick whom to ask). She decides whether to release you.
- While the request is open the row says **Pending**. In the task's details use **Ask her again** or **Cancel request**.

## Take back a mark

If your Domina marked a task done in the chat today and it was not done, it appears under your open tasks with **Marked done in chat today**. Tap **Take back** and the task is open again. Only today's marks can be taken back.

## Good to know

- A deadline is a soft target. It shapes reminders and when a task reads as overdue.
- Only a task with a deadline gets a reminder: shortly before it, and again each day while it is overdue. Reminders arrive as a push when [notifications](notifications.md) are on.
- Finishing tasks counts toward your [XP](../plans/xp-and-badges.md).

!!! note "Free plan"
    You can hold one open task at a time, and every task is a self-check task. **She confirms it** and more open tasks need Premium or Premium Plus.
