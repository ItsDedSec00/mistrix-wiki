# Findom

With Findom on, a Domina can ask you for Charms as tribute. Tribute is paid in Charms only and is not refunded. You answer every demand: pay, refuse, or press Stop Findom.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Switch and limits: **Features** > **Findom**. Demands appear as cards in the chat.

## How it works

- Findom is off until you switch on **Findom mode**. Then any Domina you chat with may demand Charms.
- A demand is a card that says **Tribute demanded.** It shows the **Amount**, **Your wallet**, **Free charms today** when you have some, and how many minutes are left before it expires.
- **Pay** takes your free charms for today first, then your wallet. If together they cannot cover the amount, the button becomes **Buy Charms**.
- **Refuse** is allowed. Her mood drops and she may answer in her own way.
- A demand lapses if you do not answer in time.
- While a demand is open, the message box waits: **Pay or refuse the demand to continue**.
- A Domina cannot demand more than you hold in your wallet plus today's free charms. If she tries, you see a note such as **Not enough Charms for this demand** with a **Buy Charms** button instead of a demand.

## Stop Findom

Every demand has a red **Stop Findom** button. Confirm **Stop Findom** and Findom ends at once, with no tribute and no punishment. Open demands are withdrawn. You can switch it on again whenever you want. More on [Stop Findom](../safety/stop-findom.md).

## Steps

1. Open **Features** > **Findom** and switch on **Findom mode**.
2. Tap **Limits** and set **Max per demand** and **Daily ceiling**. The fields show your current values.
3. Tap **Buy Charms** there if you want to top up. See [Charms](../plans/charms.md).

## Switch it off

Switch off **Findom mode** and confirm **Switch off Findom**. No Domina demands Charms from you any more and an open demand is withdrawn. This is not a refusal: nothing is held against you.

## Good to know

- **Max per demand** is a hard cap on a single demand. **Daily ceiling** is the total a Domina may demand in any 24 hours. A card says **Reduced to your own limit.** when your cap changed the amount.
- You can lower your limits at any time.
- Your free daily charms are shown on the card and count toward paying.
