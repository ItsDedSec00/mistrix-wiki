# Companion app

The Android companion app is Mistrix inside a phone app. It can let a Domina reach beyond the chat: write to you when the app is closed, change your wallpaper, cover your screen and keep apps out of reach. Each part has its own switch.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Companion app**. The row only appears inside the Android app, under **This phone**.

!!! note "Availability"
    The app is no longer offered for download from Mistrix. This page is for people who already have it installed. Their settings stay as they are.

## How it works

- Opening Mistrix in the app registers the phone. There is no extra connect step.
- Under **This phone** you choose what she may do:
    - **Notifications**: she can reach you when the app is closed.
    - **Wallpaper**: she can change your home screen.
    - **Take over the screen**: she can cover what you are doing until you answer.
    - **App blocking**: she can put apps out of reach.
    - **Web filter**: blocks adult sites in every browser on this phone.
    - **Run in the background**: turn it off and nothing is watched or blocked, and she cannot reach the phone while the app is closed.
- Some switches need an Android permission first: **Display over other apps**, **Usage access**, or the VPN permission for the web filter. The app sends you to Android settings and you come back to switch it on.
- A status line tells you what the phone is doing: **She is watching**, **Nothing is blocked**, **She let you through** or **She cannot see**. Below it, **Last instruction** shows what she last asked and whether the phone carried it out or refused.

## Settings per capability

- **Take over the screen**: **How often** (**Rarely**, **Sometimes**, **Often**), **Also when you are away**, **Let it be dismissed**, **It holds for**, and **Her picture behind it**. **Send one to this phone** tests it.
- **Wallpaper**: **How often**, **Which galleries she may take from** (her portraits, **Your own gallery**), **Put yours back afterwards**, **Hers stays for**, and **Your own wallpaper**.
- **App blocking**: choose the apps she keeps out of reach, then **How you get back in**: **Let them ask**, **Earn it** (finish today's routines) or **Let them pay** (Charms). Set a **Daily allowance** and the **Time she grants**. The app warns that with all three off, a blocked app has no way out until you change it here.
- **Web filter**: you can list sites it always lets through. **Also block encrypted DNS** closes a way around it and can break some apps.
- **Whose phone this is**: **Whoever I am with**, or one Domina you pick. That Domina is the one who takes over the screen and writes to the phone. It appears when you have more than one to choose from.

## Switch things off

Turn a switch off at any time. Switching **App blocking** off releases the blocked apps. **Export a debug report** (under **Something wrong**) makes a file you can send to support. It includes the text of your conversations, so read it first.

## Good to know

- Her notifications are written the moment she sends them, so the app opens your encrypted history in the background and keeps your PIN unlocked for two days at a time. See [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- App blocking only sees which app is in front, never what is on your screen.
- Take care with **Also when you are away**: she can cover whatever is in front of you, including a map you drive with.
