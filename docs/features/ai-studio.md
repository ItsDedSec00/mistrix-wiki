# AI Studio

AI Studio makes images from your description, and short clips from an image. Everything you make goes into your own gallery. Nothing is shared.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **AI Studio** in the sidebar, or in the bottom bar if you added it under **You** > **App** > **Bottom bar**.

## How it works

- Switch between **Image** and **Video** at the top. Describe the scene or, for video, the motion.
- **Image** can start from nothing (**Text**) or from a reference: one of your own images, the portrait of your own Domina, or the portrait of a preset Domina you can open (**Presets**). You cannot upload other pictures.
- **Video** always starts from an image: one of your own images or a Domina portrait. The clip starts from that image.
- The chips next to **Generate** pick the style. For images each shows **After free** and its price. For video the chip shows the Charm price, the length and the resolution.
- **Generate (free)** uses your free daily charms. When they are gone it reads **Generate (spend N)** and uses your wallet. If you are short, **Generate** is greyed out and the Charms chip reads **Buy Charms**. The free daily charms are shared with pictures in the chat. See [Charms](../plans/charms.md).
- A failed generation is refunded.

## Steps

1. Open **AI Studio** and choose **Image** or **Video**.
2. Pick a reference if you want one.
3. Write your description and tap **Generate**.
4. On the preview, tap **Save** to keep it as generated, or **Enhance & Save** to raise the resolution first. **Regenerate** tries again and says whether it is free or what it costs.
5. Find it later under **Recent** (images) or **Videos**.

Open an image for **Remix** (new image from it), **Video Remix** (animate it), **Download** and delete. The info button shows your text and the style used.

## Videos

A clip takes a few minutes. You see it under **Generating videos** while it runs, and you get a note when it is ready. If it fails, your charge is refunded and the card says so. You can run a few at once. Tiles play when you hover over them.

## Good to know

- A preview is not in your gallery until you tap **Save**.
- Every image Mistrix creates is labelled as AI-generated in its file details. The label stays when you download it. Please do not remove it.
- Some content cannot be generated. If your text is declined, nothing is charged and the app says what it will not create. See the [Terms](https://app.mistrix.ai/terms-of-service.html). If the check cannot run, nothing is generated or charged.
- Deleting an image or a clip cannot be undone.

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    **Enhance & Save** and your own Domina's portrait as a reference. On the Free plan **Enhance** shows locked, **Save** works as normal, and the **Presets** list holds the Dominas the Free plan can open.
