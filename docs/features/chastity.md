# Chastity

Chastity connects your Chaster account to Mistrix. Your Domina can read your lock and, within the limits you set, change it. You can hand the lock back whenever you want.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Features** > **Chastity**. The lock also shows in the **Chat menu** and at the top of **Routines**.

!!! warning "Keep your own way out"
    Mistrix cannot open a physical lock, not in an emergency and not on request. Always keep an emergency key, bolt cutters or a spare combination within reach.

## How it works

- Tap **Connect Chaster.app** and sign in at Chaster. **Chastity tracking** is on after you connect. While it is on, every Domina you chat with can read your lock and use the tools you allow below.
- When your Domina changes your lock in the chat, the change is made on Chaster, inside your limits.
- If a change does not go through, a small red line under her message says what was not applied and why, for example **Not applied on Chaster: Unlocking. Reason: not allowed for this lock.** She learns it on her next reply.
- The lock shows in the **Chat menu** as **Locked** with the time left, **Frozen**, or **Time hidden** when the timer is hidden.

## Steps

1. Under **Features** > **Chastity**, tap **Connect Chaster.app** and finish the sign-in at Chaster.
2. Check that **Chastity tracking** is on. Starting or handing over a lock needs it.
3. Pick how the lock starts:
    - **Start a new chastity lock**: Mistrix creates it and becomes the keyholder. You confirm with **I understand, lock me**. Trust on the lock is permanent and cannot be undone: you cannot unlock yourself before the timer ends.
    - **Use an existing lock**: **Hand over** makes Mistrix the keyholder, with the same permanent trust. **Link read-only** is for locks Mistrix cannot hold. Your Domina sees the lock and its status but cannot change anything. **Unlink** removes it.
4. Open **Settings** to set what she may do.

## Settings

Under **Settings** > **Domina permissions**. When you first connect, **Allow add time** is on and every other permission is off:

- **Min time per adjustment** and **Max time per adjustment**
- **Allow add time** and **Allow remove time**
- **Allow freeze / unfreeze**
- **Allow early release**
- **Allow hide timer**
- **Allow Pillory** and **Allow Hygiene Break**: these need the matching extension on the lock. A label shows **Available**, **Cooldown** or **Attaches on first use**.

## Hand the lock back

- **Chat menu** > **Hand my lock back**. It appears while Mistrix holds your key. The lock becomes a self-lock again: still running, but yours. There is nobody to ask and no reason to give.
- Switching **Chastity tracking** off also hands the lock back. Your Chaster account stays linked.
- If tracking is off but Mistrix still holds the key, a box says **MISTRIX still holds your key** with a **Hand my lock back** button.
- **Disconnect Chaster** hands the lock back, resets your Chastity settings and deletes the action history. Your device is not opened.
- Your [safe word](../safety/safe-word.md) stops a scene and does not touch the lock. Handing it back is its own choice.

## Feature history

The clock icon on **Features** opens **Feature history**: lock changes from the last 30 days with who made them. A failed change shows **failed:** and the reason. While the timer is hidden, amounts are hidden too.

## Good to know

- When Chaster refuses something, for example a second lock on a free Chaster account or an account that is not verified yet, Mistrix shows Chaster's reason in a dialog.
- A frozen timer shows the time it had when it froze.

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    **Allow freeze / unfreeze** is part of Premium. Every other permission is free.
