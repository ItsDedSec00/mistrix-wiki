# Notifications

Notifications let a Domina reach you when Mistrix is closed: reminders, a time she promised, and a heads-up when she has written first. They are off until you turn them on, per device.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **App** > **Notifications**. The Dashboard also shows a **Turn on notifications** card while they are off.

## How it works

- Switch **Notifications** on and allow the permission your browser asks for. The row reads **On**, **Off** or **Not registered**.
- You can get: reminders for routines and tasks that are still due, a message at a time she promised ("I will check on you at eight"), a heads-up with only her name when she has written to you first, and now and then a short nudge when you have been away for a while.
- **Frequency** sets the pace for each Domina separately:
    - **Low**: at most one push per day from each, with long quiet stretches.
    - **Normal**: a few pushes per day from each at most, never right after you were active.
    - **High**: up to five pushes per day from each, with shorter pauses.
- Messages from different Dominas are spaced out, so two never arrive at the same moment.
- **Quiet hours** keep her silent between the two hours you pick, in your time. A reminder for a time you were given, such as a routine reminder or a promised time, arrives at that time.
- **Delivery check** > **Send test** sends one to this device right now.

## Steps

1. Open **You** > **App** and switch **Notifications** on.
2. Allow notifications when the browser asks.
3. Choose **Frequency** and your **Quiet hours**.
4. Tap **Send test**. If you see it, you are set.

## Per Domina

In **Chat menu** > **Settings**, each Domina has two switches: **She may write first** and **Notifications from her** (**He** and **him** for a Dom). They apply to that Domina only. See [Contact](../dominas/contact.md).

## Good to know

- On iPhone and iPad the switch appears once Mistrix is on your home screen. In Safari tap Share, **Add to Home Screen**, then open Mistrix from the new icon. It needs iOS 16.4 or newer.
- No popup? Your browser may have tucked the request behind the bell icon at the right end of the address bar. Click it and choose **Allow**.
- **Not registered** means the browser allows notifications but this device is not registered with Mistrix. Tap **Register this device**.
- Brave: allow **Use Google services for push messaging** at brave://settings/privacy, restart Brave fully, then turn notifications on again.
- **Send test** says the server has no subscription, the subscription expired, or it was sent. If it was sent and nothing appears, your system is hiding it: check the notification settings for the app or browser, and focus modes.
- Reminders are lined up when you open the chat that day.

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    **High** frequency. **Low** and **Normal** are on every plan.
