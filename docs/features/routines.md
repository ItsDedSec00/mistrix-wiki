# Routines

A routine is something you do again and again: on set weekdays, or a number of times a week. Your Domina can set one for you in the chat, and you can add your own.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Routines** in the sidebar or the bottom bar. Routines due today are listed under **Today's tasks**.

## How it works

- The screen shows your Chastity status first, then your one-time [tasks](tasks.md), then **Today's tasks** (routines due today, with a counter such as 2/3) and **Future tasks** (the rest).
- A routine your Domina offers in the chat arrives as a card. Tap **Add to my routines** to keep it, or **Not now**. See [Cards](../chat/cards.md).
- Tap the box in front of a routine to mark today done, if it is a [self-check](../help/glossary.md#self-check) routine. Tap the title to open its details.
- A routine that says **she verifies** is marked done by your Domina when you report in the chat. The detail sheet then offers **Tell (name) you did it**.
- Each routine shows who owns it. **All Dominas** means every Domina you keep sees it. A Domina's name means only that Domina sees it, mentions it or releases you from it. Tap the label to change it, then confirm.
- **Suspend this week** pauses a routine until next week. **Resume this week** undoes it.

## Steps

1. Open **Routines** and tap **Add routine**.
2. Enter a **Title** and an **Instruction**.
3. Under **How often**, choose **Specific weekdays** or **Times per week**.
4. Optional: pick a **Time of day**: **Anytime**, **Morning**, **Afternoon**, **Evening** or **Night**.
5. Under **Completion**, choose **I mark it done** or **Only my Domina**.
6. Tap **Create routine**.

## Good to know

- A routine is locked after you create it. To change or remove one, tap **Request change** or **Request removal** in its details and give a reason. **Send to Domina** opens the chat with your request. For a routine shared with **All Dominas** you first pick which Domina to ask. She decides. When she approves a change, the sheet offers **Edit routine - she approved**. While a request is pending you can use **Ask her again** or **Cancel request**.
- If your Domina marked a routine done in the chat today and it was not done, open it and tap **Take back today's mark**. Marks from earlier days stay.
- Each routine gets at most one reminder a day, inside its part of day, and only if it is still due and not done. Reminders are lined up when you open the chat that day and arrive as a push when [notifications](notifications.md) are on.
- When you reach your routine limit the app tells you how many you hold and what your plan allows.
- Finishing routines counts toward your [XP](../plans/xp-and-badges.md).

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    **Only my Domina** completion, the full 4-week history in a routine's details, and a higher routine limit. On the Free plan every routine is a self-check routine and the details show the current week only.
