# Expose

With Expose on, your Domina can put one of your secrets on a board: a photo you sent in the chat or something she remembers about you, with her caption and her comment. The board is private and simulated. Nobody but you ever sees it.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    The board: **Expose** in the sidebar or the bottom bar. The settings: **Features** > **Expose**.

## How it works

- Expose is off until you switch on **Expose feature**. While it is off, no Domina posts anything about you.
- When your Domina says in the chat that she puts something on the board, the post appears on your board. A line under her message confirms it, for example **She posted about you on your Expose board.**
- A post can hold a photo, memories she quotes, her caption and her comment, as far as you allowed them in the settings. In the story she may speak as if others could see the board. They cannot.
- A post stays until she takes it down, you delete it, or its photo no longer exists. Posts she took down stay under **History**.
- If Mistrix refuses a post, nothing goes up and a line under her message says so.

## Steps

1. Open **Features** > **Expose** and switch on **Expose feature**.
2. Tap **Settings** to choose what she may use. Both options start off:
    - **Allow images**: any photo you sent her in the chat.
    - **Allow memories**: things she remembers about you. Choose the categories **Observations**, **Preferences**, **Personal** (high sensitivity) and **Milestones**.
3. Open the **Expose** board to see your posts.

## Delete a post

Tap the delete icon (**Delete post**) on a post and confirm **Delete**. It disappears from your board for good.

## Switch it off

Switch off **Expose feature** and confirm **Switch off Expose**. No Domina posts about you any more. Posts already on your board stay until you delete them. You can switch it on again later.

## Good to know

- With Expose off, the board only explains the feature and offers **Open Expose settings**.
- The board needs your PIN. Posts are stored encrypted with your PIN, like your chats. See [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- Settings are yours at any time. Nothing locks them.
