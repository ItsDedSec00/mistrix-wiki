# Features

Features are the optional parts of Mistrix. Expose and Findom are off until you turn them on, Chastity does nothing until you connect Chaster, and you can turn any of them off again at any time. Your Domina or Dom never turns one off for you.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Features** in the sidebar, or in the bottom bar if you added it under **You** > **App** > **Bottom bar**.

The **Features** screen lists **Chastity** (**On** once Chaster is connected), **Expose** and **Findom** with their current state (**On** or **Off**). The clock icon opens **Feature history**. Routines, AI Studio and notifications have their own places in the app and are described here too.

## Pages

- [Routines](routines.md): rituals that repeat on days you choose, with a daily check-off list and reminders.
- [Tasks](tasks.md): one-time obligations with an optional deadline, and how to take back a mark you did not earn.
- [Chastity](chastity.md): link your Chaster lock and decide what your Domina may change.
- [Expose](expose.md): a private board where your Domina puts your secrets on display. Only you see it.
- [Findom](findom.md): tribute cards, Charms, and the Stop Findom button.
- [AI Studio](ai-studio.md): make images and short clips from your own images and Domina portraits.
- [Notifications](notifications.md): push messages, how often, quiet hours, and a delivery check.
