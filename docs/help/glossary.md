# Glossary

The words Mistrix uses, in one or two lines each. Follow the link for the full page.

## A

### Age check
A one-time face check, with a manual review as the fallback, where the law requires age verification. It stays valid for 30 days. [Age check](../start/age-check.md)

### AI Studio
Where you make images from a description and short clips from an image. What you make stays in your own gallery. [AI Studio](../features/ai-studio.md)

## B

### Badge
The ring next to your level. It changes as you reach certain levels, from white to bronze, silver and gold. [XP & Badges](../plans/xp-and-badges.md)

## C

### Card
A small box from Mistrix inside the conversation. It asks for your yes or no, or tells you what happened. [Cards](../chat/cards.md)

### Charms
The in-app currency. You spend them on pictures, AI Studio, tribute and extra messages. They have no value outside the app. [Charms](../plans/charms.md)

### Chaster
The separate chastity app you can connect to Mistrix. Your Domina can then read your lock and change it within the limits you set. [Chastity](../features/chastity.md)

### Chat menu
The button at the top right of the chat. Its panel, headed **Overview**, holds the tabs and the **Safe word** button. [Chat](../chat/index.md)

### Context
How much of your recent conversation your Domina keeps in view word for word. Older parts are folded into memory. [Context](../memory/context.md)

### Continue
The arrow that replaces **Send** when the message box is empty. It asks your Domina to carry on without you typing. [Messages](../chat/messages.md)

## D

### Data Privacy
Three choices about your data under **You**: sharing chat data to improve Mistrix, anonymised training data, and your username on leaderboards. [Account](account.md#data-privacy-choices)

### Day Pass
On the Free plan, unlimited messages until midnight, paid with Charms from your wallet. [Free](../plans/free.md)

### Dom
A male character. Everything a Domina can do, a Dom can do too. The app's labels read "he" and "him" for a Dom. [Presets](../dominas/presets.md)

### Domina
The character you chat with. Mistrix has Dominas, Doms and Trans Dominas, as presets or built by you. [Dominas](../dominas/index.md)

## E

### Enhance
Raises the resolution of a picture before it is saved in AI Studio. Premium and Premium Plus. [AI Studio](../features/ai-studio.md)

### Example Message
A field on the **Personality** tab: a message in the voice you want. Your Domina matches its register and length. [Personality](../dominas/personality.md)

### Expose board
A private, simulated board where your Domina can put one of your secrets on display. Only you ever see it. [Expose](../features/expose.md)

## F

### Feature history
The list of lock changes from the last 30 days, with who made them. It opens from the clock icon on **Features**. [Chastity](../features/chastity.md#feature-history)

### Fetish Map
Your list of interests. For each one you choose how often it comes up, or mark it as a hard limit. [Fetish Map](../memory/fetish-map.md)

### Findom
An optional feature where a Domina may ask you for Charms as tribute. Off until you turn it on. [Findom](../features/findom.md)

### Free charms
The Charms your plan gives you each day. They refill once a day and cannot be saved up. [Charms](../plans/charms.md)

## G

### Global journal entry
A journal entry for **All Dominas**. Every Domina recalls it, and resetting one chat keeps it. [Journal](../memory/journal.md)

## H

### Hand my lock back
The button that gives your Chaster lock back to you. The lock keeps running, but you are its keyholder again. [Chastity](../features/chastity.md#hand-the-lock-back)

### Hard limit
Something you never want to happen, marked with the cross on the Fetish Map. It ranks above your goal and your rules. [Hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md)

## I

### In a scene / Just talking
The line in the **Chat menu** that says whether your Domina is leading play right now or simply talking with you. It is not a switch. [Messages](../chat/messages.md#in-a-scene-or-just-talking)

### Intensity
Part of how a character was built: how severe their voice is. It is not the same as Severity. [Your own Domina](../dominas/your-own-domina.md)

### Inventory
The toys, clothing and accessories you own. Your Domina is given the names of the items you switch on. [Inventory](../memory/inventory.md)

## J

### Journal
Notes you write yourself. Your Domina treats them as things they simply know, when a keyword comes up or when an entry is pinned. [Journal](../memory/journal.md)

## K

### Key Memories
A short text on the **Personality** tab about what your Domina should keep in mind and where you want things to go. Soft direction, not an order. [Key Memories](../memory/key-memories.md)

### Keyholder
Whoever holds the key to your Chaster lock. When Mistrix is the keyholder, your Domina can change the lock within your limits. [Chastity](../features/chastity.md)

## L

### Level
Your place on the experience ladder, 0 to 100. It does not change how demanding your Domina is. [XP & Badges](../plans/xp-and-badges.md)

### Logs you sent
Logs you chose to send with a rating or a report. Each is deleted after 90 days, and you can delete them all under **You** > **Data Privacy**. [Feedback](../chat/feedback.md)

## M

### Memory
What a Domina keeps about you: short lines in four groups and a summary of each older stretch of conversation. You can read and correct every line. [How she remembers](../memory/index.md)

### Message meter
On the Free plan, messages come from an allowance that refills through the day. Paid plans have no meter. [Free](../plans/free.md)

### Mood
How a Domina feels about you right now: **Pleased**, **Neutral** or **Displeased**. It drifts back to neutral over time. [Mood](../dominas/mood.md)

## P

### PIN
A number only you know. It encrypts your private data on your device. If you forget it, that data cannot be recovered. [PIN](../start/pin.md)

### Preset
A ready-made Domina, Dom or Trans Domina. Some are open on every plan, the **Specialists** need Premium. [Presets](../dominas/presets.md)

## Q

### Quiet hours
Two hours you pick between which notifications stay silent, in your time. [Notifications](../features/notifications.md)

## R

### Regenerate
Asks for a new reply to the same message. [Edit & Regenerate](../chat/edit-and-regenerate.md)

### Reset chat & memories
At the bottom of the **Chat menu**. It erases one Domina's conversation and what that Domina remembers. It cannot be undone. [How she remembers](../memory/index.md)

### Response Directive
Standing rules for every reply, one per line, on the **Personality** tab. Only your hard limits and safe word rank higher. [Response Directive](../memory/response-directive.md)

### Resting
A Domina you built is kept but rests while you are on the Free plan. Upgrade and everything comes back. [Your own Domina](../dominas/your-own-domina.md)

### Routine
Something you do again and again, on set weekdays or a number of times a week. [Routines](../features/routines.md)

## S

### Safe word
Stops everything in the chat at once. Press **Safe word** in the **Chat menu**, or type your own word on its own. [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md)

### Scene setting
A field on the **Personality** tab: where your Domina is while you talk. [Personality](../dominas/personality.md)

### Self-check
A routine or task you tick off yourself. On the Free plan every routine and task is self-check. [Routines](../features/routines.md)

### Severity
Your own setting, per Domina, for how demanding they are with you, from **Gentle** to **Relentless**. [Severity](../dominas/severity.md)

### Shop
Where you buy Charms, change your plan and find your invite link. [Shop](../plans/shop.md)

### She may write first
A switch per Domina that lets them write to you without being asked. [Contact](../dominas/contact.md)

### Specialists
Presets built around one focus. They are open on Premium and Premium Plus. [Presets](../dominas/presets.md)

### Stop Findom
The safe word for money. It ends Findom at once, withdraws every open demand and has no consequence. [Stop Findom](../safety/stop-findom.md)

## T

### Task
A single thing to do, once, with an optional deadline. [Tasks](../features/tasks.md)

### Timer
The countdown chip at the top right of the chat for something meant to last a set time. [Timers](../chat/timers.md)

### Trans Domina
A trans character. The app's labels read "she" and "her" for a Trans Domina. [Presets](../dominas/presets.md)

### Tribute
Charms a Domina asks for while Findom is on. You can pay, refuse or press **Stop Findom**. [Findom](../features/findom.md)

## W

### Wallet
The Charms you bought, earned through referrals or got back as a refund. Free charms are separate. [Charms](../plans/charms.md)

### Warning line
A short line with a warning sign under a reply. It is from Mistrix, not from your Domina. [Messages](../chat/messages.md#the-warning-line)

## X

### XP
Experience points you earn by chatting and finishing things. They move you up the levels. [XP & Badges](../plans/xp-and-badges.md)

## Y

### You
The account screen in the app: profile, preferences, privacy, plan and help. [Account](account.md)

### Your own Domina
A character you build yourself on Premium or Premium Plus. [Your own Domina](../dominas/your-own-domina.md)
