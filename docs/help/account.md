# Account

The **You** screen holds everything about your account in one place: profile, preferences, privacy, plan and help.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** (bottom bar on a phone, your name at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer)

## How it works

The screen reads from top to bottom like this:

| Section | What is there | More |
|---|---|---|
| Top card | Your badge, level and progress | [XP and badges](../plans/xp-and-badges.md) |
| Profile card | Tap it to change your name, gender, age and more | [Your profile](../memory/your-profile.md) |
| **Preferences** | **Fetish map**, **Inventory**, **Safe word** | [Fetish Map](../memory/fetish-map.md), [Inventory](../memory/inventory.md), [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md) |

| **Data Privacy** | Three switches about your data | [below](#data-privacy-choices) |
| **Plan** | **Subscription** (opens your subscription page on a paid plan, the plans on Free), **Buy charms** | [Plans](../plans/index.md), [Shop](../plans/shop.md) |
| **Community & About** | **Help & support**, **Suggestions**, **What's new**, **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy**, **Sign out**, **Sign out everywhere** | below |

## Username and display name

- Your **username** is permanent. You cannot change it after you register.
- Your **display name** shows on your profile card. Change it any time: tap the profile card at the top and edit **Name / How to address you**. The chat does not use it, so tell your Domina what to call you. See [Your profile](../memory/your-profile.md).
- When you have a display name, your username shows under it next to your plan.

## Account ID

- Your account ID is the long code at the very bottom of the screen.
- Tap it to copy it. Give it to support when you ask for help.

## Password

- Tap **Reset password**. A reset email goes to the address you signed up with, and the row reads **Reset email sent**.
- Follow the link in the email to choose a new password.
- Changing your password does not change your PIN. They are separate.

## Signing out

- **Sign out** signs you out on this device. Next time you sign in you unlock with your PIN again.
- **Sign out everywhere** signs out every device signed in to your account, this one included. Nothing is deleted: your chats, images and PIN stay as they are.
- After a lost or shared device, use **Sign out everywhere**, then change your PIN so your encrypted data gets a new key.

## Data privacy choices

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Data Privacy**

You choose these when you sign up and can change them here at any time. A change applies when you tap the switch. On the sign-up screen the first two switches are set to off and the leaderboard switch to on, and you can change each before you continue.

- **Share session data to improve Mistrix**: what you write, what your Domina writes back and what the app makes of it is kept so the chat can be improved.
- **Allow anonymised session data for model training**: lets anonymised conversations be used for training. It needs the first switch and turns off with it.
- **Show my username on leaderboards**: your username appears on the leaderboards in Mistrix and on the Mistrix Discord server. When off, you appear as Anonymous.
- **Logs you sent**: this row only appears once you have sent a log with a report or a rating of a message. Each log is deleted after 90 days, and tapping the row deletes them all now. Your reports and ratings stay.

What happens to your data in general is on the [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md) page.

## Help and support

- **Help & support** opens a choice of Discord or email at info@dominated.dev.
- **Suggestions** is where you send ideas.
- **What's new** lists recent changes.

## Good to know

- Some chat settings are stored with you and follow you to any device. Others stay on the device where you set them. The chat menu says which.
- Deleting your account and downloading your data both start from this screen: [Delete account](delete-account.md), [Data export](data-export.md).
