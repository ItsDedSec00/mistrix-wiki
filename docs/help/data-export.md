# Data export

You can download a copy of the personal data Mistrix holds about you. It is one file you can keep or hand to someone else.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Privacy & Security** > **Export my data**

## Steps

1. Open **You** and tap **Export my data**. The row reads **Preparing export…** while the file is built.
2. The file downloads to your device and the app says **Your data has been downloaded.**
3. If the app says **Export failed. Please try again.** or **Your data could not be saved to this device.**, check your connection and free space, then try again.

## What you get

- One `.json` file. Its name has your username and the date.
- Readable inside it: your account and profile details, what your Dominas keep about you (profile, summaries and older notes), your journal entries, your Charm history, your feature settings, your XP history and your data privacy choices.
- Your pictures and clips are listed, but the picture files themselves are not inside the export. Write to info@dominated.dev if you want them.
- Credentials such as the tokens that link your Chaster account or your devices are left out on purpose.

## Good to know

- Anything protected by your PIN, such as your chat history and the text of your routines and tasks, comes as unreadable text. Only your PIN opens it, and the server cannot. See [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- The file contains personal details. Keep it somewhere private.
- Want a different kind of request, such as a correction? Write to info@dominated.dev. We answer within one month.
- Downloading your data does not delete anything. To delete your account, see [Delete account](delete-account.md).
