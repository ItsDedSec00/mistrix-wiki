# FAQ

Short answers. Each one links to the page with the details.

## Basics

### Is Mistrix free?
Yes, there is a Free plan. Premium and Premium Plus are paid monthly plans that open more. See [Plans](../plans/index.md).

### Who am I talking to?
An AI character. Every reply is AI-generated and can contain errors, so check every directive before acting on it. The chat shows this notice under the message box. See [Mistrix in 5 minutes](../start/index.md).

### Can my Domina see or hear me?
No. The chat is text. They work from what you write, from a description of the photos you send, and from what you set up in the app, such as your limits and routines. See [Messages](../chat/messages.md).

### Can I change my Domina?
Yes. Each Domina has a separate chat, memories and settings. Some are Premium and show as locked on the Free plan. See [Dominas](../chat/choosing-a-domina.md).

### Why did my Domina write to me first?
Your Domina can write first after a quiet stretch, at a time she promised, or when a routine is due. Switch it off per Domina under **Chat menu** > **Settings** > **She may write first** (it reads **He may write first** for a male Dom). See [Contact](../dominas/contact.md).

## Control and safety

### How do I stop?
Press **Safe word** in the chat menu, or type your safe word on its own as a message. See [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md).

### Can I switch things off?
Yes, any time. Chastity, Expose and Findom each have a switch under **Features**. See [Turning things off](../safety/turning-things-off.md).

### My Domina asks for Charms. Do I have to pay?
No. Press **Stop Findom** on the demand. It ends Findom now: no tribute, no punishment. You can switch it on again any time. See [Stop Findom](../safety/stop-findom.md).

### How do I keep something out of the chat?
Set it as a hard limit. See [Hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md).

### Things feel too much.
Take a break. See [Not okay?](../safety/not-okay.md).

## PIN and privacy

### Why do I need a PIN?
Your private data is encrypted with it, and it is asked for again from time to time as part of the age check. See [PIN](../start/pin.md).

### I forgot my PIN.
It cannot be recovered. The only way back in deletes your encrypted data. See [Fix a problem](fix-a-problem.md#i-forgot-my-pin).

### Who can read my chats?
Your chat history is encrypted with your PIN. What the server can and cannot read is described on one page. See [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).

### Can I use Mistrix on more than one device?
Yes. Sign in and unlock with your PIN. Some chat settings stay on the device you set them on, and the chat menu says which ones. See [Account](account.md).

### Does my username show up on leaderboards?
Only if you leave **Show my username on leaderboards** on. When it is off you appear as Anonymous. See [Account](account.md#data-privacy-choices) and [XP and badges](../plans/xp-and-badges.md).

## Plans and Charms

### What are Charms?
Mistrix's in-app currency. Every plan gets some free Charms each day, and you can buy more in the Shop. See [Charms](../plans/charms.md).

### How do I cancel my subscription?
On a paid plan, **You** > **Plan** > **Subscription** opens your subscription page, where you can change or cancel the plan. See [Plans](../plans/index.md).

### Do Charms expire?
They are tied to your account. When the account is deleted, left-over Charms are gone. See [Delete account](delete-account.md).

## Your account

### Can I change my username?
No, it is permanent. You can change your display name any time. See [Account](account.md#username-and-display-name).

### Can I get a copy of my data?
Yes. **You** > **Privacy & Security** > **Export my data**. See [Data export](data-export.md).

### How do I delete my account?
**You** > **Privacy & Security** > **Delete account**. You have 30 days to change your mind. See [Delete account](delete-account.md).

### Is there an app?
Mistrix runs in your browser and you can add it to your home screen. On iPhone and iPad you need to add it to the home screen to get notifications. See [Notifications](../features/notifications.md) and [Companion app](../features/companion-app.md).
