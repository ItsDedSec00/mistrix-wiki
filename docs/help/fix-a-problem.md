# Fix a problem

Find your problem, try the fix, follow the link if you need more.

!!! warning "Not a technical problem?"
    If something in the chat is too much, press **Safe word** in the chat menu. More on the [Not okay?](../safety/not-okay.md) page.

## I get no notifications

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **App** > **Notifications**

- The row must say **On**. If it says **Off**, tap it and allow notifications in your browser.
- If it says **Not registered**, tap **Register this device**. Turning notifications off and on again also re-registers the device.
- Tap **Send test** under **Delivery check**. If the app says it was sent but nothing shows, your device is hiding it. Check the notification settings for this app on your device.
- Check **Quiet hours** on the same screen. Your Domina stays silent between the two hours you set.
- Check **Chat menu** > **Settings** > **Notifications from her** (it reads "Notifications from him" for a male Dom). Each Domina has a separate switch.
- iPhone and iPad: notifications only work for the app installed on your home screen. In Safari tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, open Mistrix from the new icon and turn notifications on there. This needs iOS 16.4 or newer.
- No popup in Edge or Chrome? The request may sit behind the bell icon at the right end of the address bar. Click it and choose Allow.
- Brave: open `brave://settings/privacy`, turn on **Use Google services for push messaging** and fully restart Brave.

More: [Notifications](../features/notifications.md).

## The camera stays black in the age check

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Age Verification** screen > **Try Again**

- Make sure nothing covers the camera.
- Turn on a light. A dark room can look black to the camera.
- Close other apps that use the camera.
- Still black? Try another browser such as Chrome or Firefox.
- "Camera access is blocked for this site"? Allow the camera with the camera or lock icon next to the address bar, then tap **Try Again**.
- "No camera was found on this device"? Sign in on your phone and verify there. Your whole account is then verified on every device for 30 days.
- "The quick check could not confirm your age"? Tap **Request Manual Review**.

More: [Age check](../start/age-check.md).

## My lock did not change

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Features** > **Chastity** > **Settings**

- When your Domina announces a change to your lock and it does not go through, a small red note appears in the chat after her message. It reads "Not applied on Chaster", then the action, then the reason.
- Reasons you can fix:
    - **not allowed for this lock**: switch on the matching permission under **Domina permissions**, for example **Allow add time**, **Allow remove time** or **Allow early release**. **Allow freeze / unfreeze** is a Premium permission.
    - **outside this lock's limits**: the change was bigger or smaller than your **Min time per adjustment** and **Max time per adjustment**. Move the sliders if you want it.
    - **no lock running**: start or link a lock first.
- Reasons you cannot fix: **no amount given** (no time was named) and **the same change was already made a short while ago**. Ask again with a clear amount if you want it.
- **Chaster did not accept it**: Chaster refused the change. Check your lock on Chaster and ask again later.
- **Chastity tracking** must be on. If you switched it off, your Domina cannot touch the lock.
- Your lock on Chaster shows the real state.

More: [Chastity](../features/chastity.md).

## My Domina forgot something

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat menu** > **Memory**

- Open the **Memory** tab and look for the fact. If it is wrong, edit the line. If it is missing, write it into the **Journal** tab or into **Key Memories** on the **Personality** tab. If it is not meant to stay, delete the line.
- Your Domina reads your edit from the next reply on.
- Under **Settings**, check that **She uses memories** is on. When it is off, your Domina answers from the conversation alone (the label says **He uses memories** for a male Dom).
- **Context** under **Settings** sets how much of your recent conversation stays in view word for word. A higher value keeps more in view.
- If something must always be followed and keeps slipping, put it into **Response Directive**.

More: [How she remembers](../memory/index.md), [Journal](../memory/journal.md), [Key Memories](../memory/key-memories.md).

## A reply did not arrive

- The app often tries again by itself. Wait a moment before you send anything again.
- If you see an error such as "The reply timed out. Try again.", send your message again, or tap **Regenerate** under the last reply.
- If you are on the Free plan and a card says **Free messages used up** with a countdown, your free messages are spent. The next one is ready when the clock runs out. See [Free plan](../plans/free.md).
- Still nothing? Check your connection, reload the app, and open the chat again.

More: [Messages](../chat/messages.md), [Edit and regenerate](../chat/edit-and-regenerate.md).

## A photo could not be read

- Under your photo the chat says "This photo could not be read." and that your Domina has not seen it. They do not know what the photo shows.
- Send it again and write a few words with it. Your Domina reads your words and the photo together.
- Try a different photo if it still fails.
- "Your photo could not be sent" or "This image could not be sent": try another photo or check your connection.

More: [Photos](../chat/photos.md).

## I forgot my PIN

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Unlock screen > **Forgot your PIN?**

- There is no way to recover a forgotten PIN. Your encrypted data is only readable with it.
- The only way back in is **Reset account & delete encrypted data**. You type DELETE and tap **Delete data & disable PIN**. This permanently deletes your chat history with every Domina, your fetishes and interests, hard limits, inventory, safe word, and your routines and open tasks.
- The screen says what stays: the memories your Dominas hold about you, your profile details and your pictures.
- Afterwards the app asks you to set a new PIN, and you set things up again.

More: [PIN](../start/pin.md).

## Wrong name or pronouns

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > your name at the top > **Name / How to address you**

- Check your gender on your profile. It decides the pronouns your Domina uses for you.
- The chat does not use **Name / How to address you**. Tell your Domina in the chat what to call you. For something that should always hold, add a line to **Response Directive**.

More: [Your profile](../memory/your-profile.md), [Response Directive](../memory/response-directive.md).
