# Delete account

You can delete your account at any time. It is erased 30 days after you ask, and until then you can still cancel.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Privacy & Security** > **Delete account**

## Steps

1. If you pay for Premium or Premium Plus, cancel the subscription first: **You** > **Plan** > **Subscription**.
2. Want a copy of your data? Download it first: [Data export](data-export.md).
3. Tap **Delete account** and read the confirmation. Tap **Confirm**.
4. The app says **Account scheduled for deletion** and shows the date.

## The 30 days

- A banner at the top of **You** shows the date and the days left.
- You can keep using Mistrix as usual during that time.
- To change your mind, tap **Cancel Deletion** in that banner. Signing back in is enough to reach it.
- The **Delete account** row disappears while a deletion is scheduled.
- If you cannot pass the age check, you can delete your account from the **Age Verification** screen with **Delete My Account**.

## What is erased

- After the 30 days your profile, chats, images, memories and your login are erased permanently. This cannot be undone.
- Left-over Charms are gone with the account. They are not refunded.
- A linked Chaster account is disconnected. Disconnecting does not open a physical lock. See [Chastity](../features/chastity.md).
- Payment records are kept as the law requires. Copies in backups are gone within 44 days of the deletion. Details are in the Privacy Policy, which you open from **You** > **Community & About** > **Privacy Policy**.

## Accounts nobody signs in to

- If you do not sign in for 12 months, your account and its data are deleted.
- We warn you by email, and by notification where you allowed them, three months before and again one month before the deletion date.
- Signing in once before that date keeps your account, and the 12 months start again.
- Accounts on a paid plan are not deleted for inactivity. Neither are accounts with a running Chastity lock held by Mistrix.

## Good to know

- Deleting the account is different from resetting one chat. To clear a single Domina's chat and memories, use **Chat menu** > **Reset chat & memories**. See [How she remembers](../memory/index.md).
- Deleted means deleted. We do not bring data back from backups.
