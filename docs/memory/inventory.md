# Inventory

Your inventory lists the toys, clothing and accessories you own. Your Domina is given the names of the items you switch on, and is told to use nothing else.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Preferences** > **Inventory**

## How it works

- Items are sorted in three tabs: **Toys**, **Clothing** and **Accessories**.
- To add an item, pick a **Subcategory** (required) and enter a **Name** (required). **Description** is optional, and so is an image link.
- **Fetish tags** are optional. They link an item to entries from the [Fetish Map](fetish-map.md) list.
- Each item has a switch. Off means it is left out for now and shows "Excluded for now".
- Changes apply the next time you open the chat.
- Items are stored with your PIN-protected data: see [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).

## Steps

1. Open **Inventory** and choose a tab.
2. Fill in the form under **Add item** and tap **+ Add Item**.
3. Use the switch, the tag icon or **x** on an item to change or remove it.

## Export and import

- **Export** saves your inventory as a file.
- **Import** reads a file you exported earlier. The app reports how many items it took and how many it skipped.

## Good to know

!!! note "Free, Premium and Premium Plus"
    Free accounts can store up to 5 items per tab. Premium and Premium Plus can store up to 30 items in total. The counter above the form shows your count and limit.

- Only names and descriptions reach your Domina. Keep names short and clear.
