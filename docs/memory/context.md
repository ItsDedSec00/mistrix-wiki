# Context

**Context** sets how much of your latest conversation your Domina keeps in view exactly as you wrote it. Older parts are folded into their [memory](index.md).

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Settings** > **Context**

## How it works

- It is a slider measured in characters. It runs from 30,000 to 120,000, and the app marks the default (60,000).
- A higher value means your Domina has more of your recent conversation in front of them. Older parts move into memory later.
- A lower value moves older parts into memory sooner.
- The setting is stored per Domina and follows you to any device.
- A change applies from your next message.

## Good to know

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    Free accounts can set up to 60,000. Above that the slider shows "Above 60,000 needs Premium."

- If you go back to Free, values above 60,000 stop applying. Your choice comes back when you upgrade again.
- Context does not delete anything. To see or change what was kept from older parts, open the **Memory** tab.
- The info mark next to **Context** repeats this in the app.
