# Key Memories

**Key Memories** is a short text for what your Domina should always keep in mind about you and where you want this to go. It is soft direction, not an order.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Personality** tab > **What you want from her** > **Key Memories**

## How it works

- Write up to 500 characters. The counter under the label shows what is left.
- The app describes it as soft direction: it tilts your Domina's tone and where they take things, and they do not quote it back to you.
- It is saved by itself a moment after you stop typing, and when you leave the field. There is no save button.
- It belongs to one Domina. Set it separately for each one.
- Changes apply from your next message.
- An empty field shows an example of what to write.

## Example

> Help me get comfortable giving up control, slowly. I earn things; nothing is given.

## Good to know

- Use [Response Directive](response-directive.md) for rules your Domina follows in every reply. Key Memories is for direction and background.
- Use the [Journal](journal.md) for facts your Domina should recall when they come up.
- **Reset chat & memories** does not clear Key Memories.
- The heading reads "What you want from him" for a male Dom.
- Where this text is stored and who can read it is on [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
