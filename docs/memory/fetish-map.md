# Fetish Map

The Fetish Map is your list of interests. For each one you choose how often your Domina should bring it into play, or mark it as a hard limit.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Preferences** > **Fetish map**. Also the **Fetish map** tile on the home screen.

## How it works

Each row has one scale. Tap one symbol per row:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cross | **Hard limit.** See [Hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md). |
| Empty circle | **Not for me.** The default. |
| One heart | **Occasionally** |
| Two hearts | **Often** |
| Three hearts | **Constantly** |

- Hearts set how often an interest is woven into your chats, not how intense it is. The page says so at the top.
- Think of it as what you do yourself, not what attracts you. "Latex" means you wear it.
- Interests are grouped in categories: General Sex Acts, Clothing & Fetish Wear, BDSM, Impact & Pain Play, Body & Worship, Power Exchange & Fantasy, Bodily Fluids and Misc Kinks. Each has a short description.
- A line at the bottom counts your interests and hard limits.
- The list is fixed. You cannot add your own entries.

## Steps

1. Open the Fetish Map from **You** or the home screen.
2. Tap a symbol on each row you care about.
3. Tap **Save & Continue**.

Changes apply the next time you open the chat. The **Fetishes** tab in the Chat menu shows your map read-only. Edit it here.

## Good to know

- **Reset AI learning** (top right) clears any adjustments made earlier on top of your map. Your hearts and hard limits stay as you set them. You confirm with **Reset learning**.
- A section called **No longer offered** appears only if you had a hard limit on an interest that has since been removed from the app. The limit stays until you remove it.
- Your map is stored with your PIN-protected data: see [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- A hard limit here is also your protection in the chat. If a Domina crosses one, see [Not okay?](../safety/not-okay.md).
