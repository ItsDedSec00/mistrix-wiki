# Response Directive

**Response Directive** holds standing rules for how your Domina answers you, one rule per line. The app describes them as binding: only your hard limits and your safe word rank higher.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Personality** tab > **What you want from her** > **Response Directive**

## How it works

- Write one rule per line. The limit is 1,500 characters in total, and the counter under the label shows what is left.
- The rules apply to every reply from that Domina.
- It is saved by itself a moment after you stop typing, and when you leave the field.
- It belongs to one Domina. Set it separately for each one.
- Changes apply from your next message.
- An empty field shows two examples to start from.

## Examples

> Always greet me before anything else.
>
> Never send me to bed.

## Good to know

- Your hard limits and your safe word always come first: [Hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md), [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md).
- For direction and background rather than rules, use [Key Memories](key-memories.md).
- Your Domina can slip, like with anything they write. If they drift from a rule, remind them in the chat and see [Fix a problem](../help/fix-a-problem.md).
- **Reset chat & memories** does not clear your Response Directive.
- Two more fields sit on the same tab: **Example Message** and **Scene setting**. They are covered under [Personality](../dominas/personality.md).
