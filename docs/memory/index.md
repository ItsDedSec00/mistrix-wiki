# How she remembers

Each Domina or Dom keeps a separate memory of you. You can read it, change any line, and switch it off.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** (top right, opens the **Overview** panel) > **Memory** tab

## How it works

- Your Domina sees your most recent messages word for word. How much stays in view is the **Context** setting: [Context](context.md).
- When the conversation grows past that, the oldest part leaves their view. It is folded into their memory first, so the main points can be kept.
- Their memory has two parts, both shown on the **Memory** tab:
    - **What she knows** (**What he knows** for a male Dom): short lines in four groups: **Rules and arrangements**, **About you**, **Between you** and **Still open**.
    - **Earlier in your conversations**: a short summary for each stretch of conversation that has left their view. It is collapsed by default.
- You may also see **Older notes**, collapsed. These are older notes from before this memory format.
- Your Domina also draws on what you set yourself: your [journal](journal.md), [Key Memories](key-memories.md), [Response Directive](response-directive.md), [Fetish Map](fetish-map.md), [inventory](inventory.md) and [profile](your-profile.md).
- Memory belongs to one Domina. What one knows, another does not, except journal entries you make global.

## Correct memory

- Hover over a line (on a phone the buttons are always visible) and use the pencil to edit it or the bin to delete it. Deleting asks you to confirm.
- You do not have to tell your Domina. They read your version from their next reply.
- If a change does not go through, for example because the line changed meanwhile, the panel shows a message and reloads. Try again.
- Wrong or old line in a summary? Edit or delete it the same way.

## Switch memory off

In the **Settings** tab, per Domina:

- **She keeps memories**: off means nothing new is written down. What is already kept stays.
- **She uses memories**: off means your Domina answers from the conversation alone. Your journal is not used either.

The labels follow your Domina, so for a male Dom they read "He keeps memories" and "He uses memories".

## Limits

- Summaries are shorter than the conversation. Small details can be lost, and your Domina can misremember or mix things up. If something is wrong, correct the line.
- Anything you delete can still reach your Domina while the messages that mention it are in their view, or if it comes up again in the chat.
- Memory is not shared between Dominas. Tell each one, or write a global [journal](journal.md) entry.
- A photo that could not be read has not been seen by your Domina: see [Photos](../chat/photos.md).
- **Reset chat & memories** (bottom of the Chat menu) erases the conversation and everything that Domina remembers in that chat. Your Key Memories, Response Directive, Severity and global journal entries stay.

## Good to know

- Memory lines are stored so your Domina can read them. What the server can and cannot read is explained once, on [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- Your memory lines and summaries are part of your [data export](../help/data-export.md).
