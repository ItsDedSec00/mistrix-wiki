# Your profile

Your profile is a short form about you. Your Domina reads the parts they need so they can talk to the person you are. Every field is optional.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > your name card at the top. The screen is called **About You**.

## Fields

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Name / How to address you** | A name or nickname. |
| **Gender** | Male, Female, Non-binary, Other or Prefer not to say. |
| **Biological Sex** | Penis, Vagina or Prefer not to say. |
| **Age** | 18 or older. |
| **Physical Limitations** | Health facts to work around: joints, heart, mobility. |
| **Backstory** | Up to 500 characters. A few sentences about your life and situation. |

## How it works

- **Gender** decides how your Domina addresses you:
    - Female: she/her, whatever your anatomy.
    - Non-binary, Other or Prefer not to say: they/them, unless you ask for something else in the chat.
    - Male or empty: nothing special is set.
- **Biological Sex** helps your Domina tailor physical directives to you.
- **Physical Limitations** tell your Domina what to work around. Keep them current, and use [Hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md) for anything that is a firm no.
- **Backstory** is sent with your messages so your Domina can read it every time you talk.
- The chat does not use your **Name**. If you want to be called something there, tell your Domina or write it in your **Backstory** or [Key Memories](key-memories.md).
- Changes apply the next time you open the chat.

## Steps

1. Open **You** and tap the name card.
2. Fill in what you like.
3. Tap **Save & Continue**, or **Skip for now**. A screen saying **You are ready.** follows, with an **Enter MISTRIX** button.

## Good to know

- The same screen appears during [Onboarding](../start/onboarding.md).
- How each part of your profile is stored is explained once, on [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- If a line in your Domina's memory uses the wrong pronouns for you, edit it in the **Memory** tab.
