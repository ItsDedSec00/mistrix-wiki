# Journal

The journal holds notes you write yourself. Your Domina treats them as things they simply know about you. Your Domina never changes an entry you wrote.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** > **Journal** tab

## How it works

- Each entry is a short note to your Domina, in your own words, in the third person. It needs 20 to 500 characters.
- An entry comes up in one of two ways:
    - **Keywords**: when a word in your message starts with one of them, the entry comes to mind. "run" also catches "running". Add up to 8, separated by commas.
    - **Pinned**: a pinned entry is always there and needs no keywords.
- An entry with neither shows "no key phrase yet: pin it or add one". It does nothing until you do.
- When your Domina used journal entries for a reply, a **What she remembered** link appears under it (for a male Dom, **What he remembered**). Tap it to see them.

## Steps

1. Open the **Journal** tab.
2. Choose who the entry is for with the switch at the top: your Domina's name, or **All Dominas**.
3. Tap **+**, fill in **Keywords** and **Description**, then tap **Add entry**.
4. Use **Pin**, **Edit**, **Make global** or **Make private**, and **Delete** on an entry later. **Delete** works at once.
5. Use the search box to find an entry.

## This Domina or all Dominas

- **Your Domina's name**: only this Domina recalls these. The tab says "Only (name) recalls these."
- **All Dominas**: every Domina recalls these. Good for things that are always true about you.
- **Make global** and **Make private** move an entry ([global entries](../help/glossary.md#global-journal-entry)) between the two.
- Resetting a chat removes that Domina's own entries. Global entries stay.

## Good to know

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    Editing an entry is part of Premium. Free accounts can add, pin and delete entries.

- A counter under the switch shows how many entries you have and your limit. Free accounts have a smaller limit than Premium.
- You can pin only a limited number of entries. The app tells you when you reach it and asks you to unpin one.
- Turning off **She uses memories** in **Settings** also stops the journal being used: see [How she remembers](index.md).
- Your journal is stored so your Domina can read it. See [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
