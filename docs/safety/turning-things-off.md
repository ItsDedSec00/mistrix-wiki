# Turning things off

Every optional feature is yours to switch on and off at any time. Your Domina never turns one off for you, and no setting is locked.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Features** shows the banner "Yours to switch". Chat switches are in **Chat menu** > **Settings**.

## Features

| What | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Chastity | **Features** > **Chastity** > **Chastity tracking** (once Chaster is connected) | Mistrix hands your lock back on Chaster. It keeps running as a self-lock and you are its keyholder again. Your Chaster account stays linked. |
| Expose | **Features** > **Expose** > **Expose feature** | No Domina posts about you any more. Posts already on your board stay until you delete them. |
| Findom | **Features** > **Findom** > **Findom mode**, or **Stop Findom** on a demand | No Domina demands Charms. An open demand is withdrawn. Nothing is held against you. |

Each switch asks once ("Switch off ...?") and then goes. Switch it on again with the same toggle.

## In the chat

- **Offer me pictures**: off removes her picture offers.
- **She may write first**: off stops her from writing to you on her own. This is per Domina.
- **Notifications from** her: off stops her reminders and heads-ups. This is per Domina.
- **She keeps memories**: off stops new memories. **She uses memories**: off makes her answer from the conversation alone.

All of these are in **Chat menu** > **Settings**. The labels use your Domina's pronoun, for example **He may write first** for a Dom.

## Elsewhere

- **Notifications** for this device: **You** > **App** > **Notifications**.
- Chastity permissions one by one (for example **Allow add time**): **Features** > **Chastity** > **Settings**.
- What Expose may use: **Features** > **Expose** > **Settings** (**Allow images**, **Allow memories**).
- Disconnect Chaster: **Features** > **Chastity** > **Disconnect Chaster**. This hands your lock back and deletes the action history. Your physical device is not opened.
- Delete a post: **Expose** > trash icon on the post.

## Good to know

- Pausing is always possible: stop writing, or close the app.
- To erase a whole conversation and what she remembers, use **Reset chat & memories** at the bottom of the chat menu. This cannot be undone.
- For an immediate stop, use [Safe word](safe-word.md).
