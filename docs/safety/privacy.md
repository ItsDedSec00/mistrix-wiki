# Privacy

Mistrix protects your most personal data with a key made from your PIN. Other data has to be readable for the app to work. This page says which is which. The [Privacy Policy](https://app.mistrix.ai/privacy-policy.html) is the full and binding text. If a page here ever differs, the policy wins.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Privacy & Security** and **Data Privacy**. **Privacy Policy** is under **Community & About**.

## Protected by your PIN

Encrypted on your device before it is sent. We store only the encrypted data and cannot read it:

- your chat history, one encrypted copy per Domina
- your Fetish map and interest levels
- your [hard limits](hard-limits.md) and [safe word](safe-word.md)
- your inventory
- your Domina's personality settings
- the paths to the pictures of your Dominas
- the posts on your Expose board

Your PIN never leaves your device. If you forget it, this data cannot be recovered, not by you and not by us. See [PIN](../start/pin.md).

## Readable by Mistrix

Encrypted on our servers with a key we control. We can decrypt it, only to run the service:

- what she remembers about you: the running profile, summaries, her notes and your journal entries
- a server copy of your hard limits, used for safety filtering
- the written descriptions of photos you send
- your Chaster login tokens, if you connect Chaster

Not encrypted in this way:

- your username and email
- your display name, gender, exact age and anatomy from **About You**
- your plan, XP and level

## While you chat

- To write a reply, the conversation and what she needs to know about you are sent over an encrypted connection to the services that write her replies. They are listed in the Privacy Policy. Your email, account identity and display name are not sent.
- A photo you send goes to an image-reading service. Both the picture and its description are deleted when you delete the conversation or your account.
- Pictures are files with their own address. Anyone who has the address of a picture can open it, so do not share it.
- We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

## Your choices

Three optional choices, asked when you register and changeable any time under **You** > **Data Privacy**:

- **Share session data to improve Mistrix**
- **Allow anonymised session data for model training** (needs the first one)
- **Show my username on leaderboards** (off shows you as Anonymous)

When you rate or report a reply you can tick a box to send your log for that one message. It is never ticked for you, and your safe word is never included.

If you report a message, that message is sent to us readable.

## Good to know

- Your PIN confirms it is still you and unlocks your data. It is asked for again at least every 7 days.
- **Export my data** downloads what we hold. See [Data export](../help/data-export.md).
- **Delete account** erases your account and data after a 30 day wait. See [Delete account](../help/delete-account.md).
