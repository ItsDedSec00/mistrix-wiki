# Safety

Everything here is yours to use at any time. Your Domina or Dom never switches a feature off for you, and nothing on these pages needs permission.

!!! warning "Need to stop right now?"
    Open the **Chat menu** and press **Safe word**. See [Safe word](safe-word.md). If a Findom demand is on screen, press **Stop Findom**. See [Stop Findom](stop-findom.md).

- [Safe word](safe-word.md): the button and the typed word, and what happens after.
- [Stop Findom](stop-findom.md): the safe word for money.
- [Hard limits](hard-limits.md): what she never crosses, and where you set it.
- [Turning things off](turning-things-off.md): every feature, and where its switch is.
- [Not okay?](not-okay.md): when something gets to be too much.
- [Privacy](privacy.md): what your PIN protects and what we can read.
