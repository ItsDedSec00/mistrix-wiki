# Not okay?

If something feels like too much, you are allowed to stop. You do not need a reason.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** (top right) > **Safe word**

## Right now

1. Press **Safe word** in the chat menu. Everything stops and she answers with care. See [Safe word](safe-word.md).
2. If a Findom demand is on screen, press **Stop Findom**. See [Stop Findom](stop-findom.md).
3. Switch off what is bothering you under **Features**. See [Turning things off](turning-things-off.md).
4. Close the app and take a break. You can come back whenever you want.

## If you feel unwell

- Stop whatever you were doing and check on yourself.
- A line under a reply, such as "If you feel unwell, stop and check now.", is a reminder to do exactly that.
- If it is a physical emergency, call your local emergency number.

## Talk to a person

Mistrix is a written, AI-driven experience. It is not counselling and it cannot help in a crisis.

- [Find a Helpline](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, verified helplines in over 175 countries.
- [Befrienders Worldwide](https://www.befrienders.org) connects emotional support centres around the world.
- Someone you trust can help too. You do not have to explain Mistrix to ask for company.

## Tell us

- **You** > **Help & support** reaches the Mistrix community on Discord or support by email.
- Under a reply, the report icon sends us that message.
