# Safe word

The safe word stops whatever is happening in the chat. Nothing is asked and nothing is held against you.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Chat** > **Chat menu** (top right) > **Safe word** (red button at the bottom of the menu)

    Set or change the word: **You** > **Preferences** > **Safe word**

## How it works

- The **Safe word** button is always in the chat menu. It does not need the word you chose.
- If she is writing a reply, the reply is cut off.
- A card appears: "Everything stops here. Take your time. There is no judgment."
- She answers with care, not with the scene.
- An open Findom demand is withdrawn. Findom itself stays on unless you switch it off. See [Stop Findom](stop-findom.md).
- Her mood towards you is reset to neutral.

## Type your own word

- You choose your safe word when you set up your account. You can change it any time.
- Typing it has the same effect as the button.
- It counts when it stands on its own: as the whole message, as its own sentence or on its own line. A word like "red" can still appear inside a normal sentence without stopping anything.
- The button always works, even if you never set a word.

## Good to know

- The safe word stops the scene and nothing more. It does not open your lock. To hand a lock back, use **Hand my lock back** in the same menu (it shows only when Mistrix holds your lock), or see [Chastity](../features/chastity.md).
- A Findom demand on screen blocks the message box until you answer it. The button in the chat menu still works. You can also press **Stop Findom**.
- If the button cannot reach the server, a message tells you to tap it again.
- To switch a feature off for good, see [Turning things off](turning-things-off.md).
