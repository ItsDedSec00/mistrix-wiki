# Stop Findom

**Stop Findom** is the safe word for money. It ends Findom now, whatever is on screen.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    On any Findom demand in the chat: red **Stop Findom** button under **Pay** (with the amount) and **Refuse**

    Switch Findom on or off later: **Features** > **Findom** > **Findom mode**

## Steps

1. Press **Stop Findom** on the demand.
2. Confirm with **Stop Findom** in the "Stop Findom?" box.
3. A note says Findom is off. The demand shows "You stopped Findom."

## How it works

- Findom switches off for your whole account, with every Domina.
- Every open demand is withdrawn.
- You pay nothing and there is no punishment.
- It is not a refusal. Your Domina treats it as your choice to end Findom.
- The message box opens again right away.
- You can switch Findom back on in **Features** whenever you like.

## Good to know

- **Refuse** is different. It leaves Findom on, shifts her mood and she may respond with consequences. See [Findom](../features/findom.md).
- A demand is never put to you for more than your Charms plus today's free charms. If she tries, you see a note that you need more Charms instead of a demand.
- While Findom is on, you can also cap what she may ask under **Features** > **Findom** > **Limits**: **Max per demand** and **Daily ceiling**.
- The [Safe word](safe-word.md) withdraws an open demand too, but leaves Findom on.
