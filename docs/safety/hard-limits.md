# Hard limits

A hard limit is something you never want to happen. It ranks above your goal and your rules.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **You** > **Preferences** > **Fetish map**, or the **Fetish map** tile on **Home**

    Set a limit with the cross (X) next to an interest.

## How it works

- Every interest on the **Fetish map** has a cross. A cross means hard limit.
- The legend reads "hard limit" for the cross. The circle means "not for me". Hearts set how often she weaves an interest into your scenes.
- Your hard limits are sent with every message, so she has them in front of her each time.
- The tasks she suggests leave out your hard limits.
- Like your safe word, they rank above your rules. The app says so on the **Response Directive** field.
- You can set or remove a limit at any time. A saved change reaches her the next time you open the chat.

## Steps

1. Open **Fetish map**.
2. Find the interest and press the cross. It turns red.
3. Press **Save & Continue**. The bottom of the screen counts your hard limits.

## Good to know

- Hard limits cover the interests on the map. For anything else, say it in the chat, or put it in your [Response Directive](../memory/response-directive.md). Those are rules she follows, and your hard limits still outrank them.
- Health facts such as bad knees go in **Physical Limitations** under [Your profile](../memory/your-profile.md). She adapts positions and durations to them.
- **Reset AI learning** on the map clears her automatic adjustments to your preferences. Your hearts and hard limits stay as you set them.
- An interest that has left the app keeps its limit until you remove it. It then appears under "No longer offered".
- She is an AI and can get things wrong. The app says so under the message box: replies may contain errors. If she crosses a limit, press [Safe word](safe-word.md), then tell her.
- Your hard limits are encrypted with your PIN. A server copy is kept for safety filtering. See [Privacy](privacy.md).
