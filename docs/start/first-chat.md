# First chat

Your chat is a written conversation with the Domina or Dom you chose. They open with a first message. You answer in your own words.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Dashboard** > **Continue conversation**, or the **Chat** tab at the bottom

## How it works

- **Their first message** is already there when the chat opens. Reply to it, or say what you want.
- **Write in the box at the bottom** ("Write to ..."). Send, and the reply appears as they write it. The header shows "typing..." while they write.
- **Replies are AI-generated** and can contain errors. A line under the box reminds you. Check any instruction before you act on it.
- **Tap their picture** in the header to see it full screen. **Back** returns to the Dashboard.
- **Under a message** you find small icons. **Edit** is on both sides. On their replies: **Regenerate**, **Good reply**, **Bad reply** and **Report this message**. On your own messages: **Delete this and everything after**. The icons disappear while a reply is being written. See [Edit & Regenerate](../chat/edit-and-regenerate.md) and [Feedback](../chat/feedback.md).

## The chat menu

Tap the menu icon (**Chat menu**) at the top right. The **Overview** opens with these tabs:

- **Personality**: who they are and what you want from them. See [Dominas](../dominas/index.md).
- **Memory**: what they know about you. See [Memory](../memory/index.md).
- **Journal**: notes you write for them to know. See [Journal](../memory/journal.md).
- **Fetishes**: your fetish map, plus what your Domina has noticed on top of it.
- **Gallery**: pictures from this chat.
- **Settings**: for example **Severity** and the time setting.

At the bottom of the menu is the red **Safe word** button. It is always there.

## Tips for the first minutes

- Say plainly what you want and what you do not. You do not need special commands.
- If a reply is wrong or too much, say so, or use **Regenerate**.
- You can type your safe word on its own at any time. It stops everything.
- Anything you are unsure about can be switched off in **Features**. See [Turning things off](../safety/turning-things-off.md).
