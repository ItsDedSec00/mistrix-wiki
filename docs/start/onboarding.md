# Onboarding

After sign-up the app walks you through a few setup screens. Everything can be changed later, and some steps can be skipped.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Opens automatically after sign-up and the [age check](age-check.md). Later, change each item under **You**.

## Steps

1. **Quiz.** Seven short questions about your experience. You get a result you can adjust before you confirm with **Confirm & Continue**. It sets where your Domina starts. How demanding they are can be changed any time in the chat. See [Severity](../dominas/severity.md).
2. **Fetish map.** Mark what you like and what you never want. The cross is a **hard limit**. More hearts mean it comes up more often. Tap **Save & Continue**. See [Fetish Map](../memory/fetish-map.md).
3. **Your Inventory.** Add toys, clothing and accessories you own. You can skip this and add items later. Tap **Continue**. See [Inventory](../memory/inventory.md).
4. **Safe Word.** Choose a word you will remember. The screen suggests a few. Tap **Set Safe Word & Continue**. See [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md).
5. **PIN.** Set your PIN. See [PIN](pin.md).
6. **About You.** All fields are optional: name or how to address you, gender, biological sex, age, physical limitations and a short backstory. Tap **Save & Continue** or **Skip for now**. See [Your profile](../memory/your-profile.md).
7. **You are ready.** A short summary of what you set. Tap **Enter MISTRIX**.
8. **Choose your Domina** (Free plan). First pick **Dominas**, **Doms** or **Trans**, then tap one to read about them and tap **Continue with** their name. See [Choosing a Domina](../chat/choosing-a-domina.md).

!!! note "Premium and Premium Plus"
    On a paid plan you design your own Domina first, and the quiz and the Domina choice are skipped. See [Your own Domina](../dominas/your-own-domina.md).

## Good to know

- **Safe word before PIN.** The PIN step needs a safe word first.
- How your answers are stored is explained once, in [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- **Hard limits** are the most important thing to set here. They apply to everything. See [Hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md).
- You can change your fetish map, inventory, safe word and profile later under **You**. The quiz is set once.
