# Sign up

You create an account with an email address or with Google. A short confirmation screen comes before the account exists.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Start screen > **Start** > **Got it** > the registration form

## Steps

1. On the start screen tap **Start**. A short "How MISTRIX works" screen follows. Tap **Got it**.
2. Fill in **Email**, **Username**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**, then tap **Create Account**. Or tap **Sign up with Google**.
3. Open the verification email and click the link. Then come back and tap **I've verified my email**. If it did not arrive, tap **Resend verification email**.
4. The confirmation screen ("One last step before your account is created") appears. Tick the three required boxes and choose your data options (below).
5. Tap **Create Account**. Where the [age check](age-check.md) applies, it comes first. Then the plans screen opens. Tap **Continue for free** to stay on the Free plan, or pick a plan.

## Good to know

- **Username:** 3 to 32 characters. Letters, numbers, `_` and `-` only. It must be unique.
- **Password:** at least 8 characters.
- **Google sign-up** skips the verification email. You only pick a username ("Almost done") and tap **Continue**.
- **The three required boxes:** you are at least 18, you accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and you understand that characters, messages and images are AI-generated and the content rules apply. The button stays disabled until all three are ticked.
- **Your data options** are separate and optional:
    - The first option lets Mistrix keep your chat data, encrypted, to improve the app. Off by default.
    - The second option allows anonymised data to be used for training. Off by default, and it needs the first option.
    - **Show my username on leaderboards.** On by default. When it is off, you appear as Anonymous.
- You can change all three later in **You** > **Data Privacy**. [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md) explains what is stored.
- If you arrive through a friend's invite link, it is applied for you. See [Referrals](../plans/referrals.md).
- Signing in later: **Sign in** on the start screen, or **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.
