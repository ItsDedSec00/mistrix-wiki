# Age check

Mistrix is for adults. Where the law requires it, you confirm your age once with a short face check. If that does not work, a manual review is the fallback.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Appears automatically after you create your account: **Age Verification** > **Start Face Check**

## How it works

- **Where it applies:** the check appears only where age verification is legally required. If it appears for you, you cannot use the app until you pass it.
- **Face check:** about 15 seconds. It runs on your own device. No image or data leaves your device, and nothing is stored. Mistrix only receives the result.
- **Valid for 30 days** on every device you use. After that you verify again.
- **Your PIN** confirms it is still you between checks. It is asked for at least every 7 days. See [PIN](pin.md).
- **No third party** is involved in the face check.

## Steps

1. Tap **Start Face Check** and allow the camera.
2. Look into the camera and turn your head once.
3. When it passes, you continue automatically.

## If the face check fails

- **Black picture:** make sure the camera is not covered, turn on a light, close other apps that use the camera, or try another browser.
- **Camera blocked:** allow the camera in your browser, using the camera or lock icon next to the address bar.
- **No camera on this device:** sign in on your phone and verify there. A QR code on the screen opens Mistrix on your phone. One check covers your whole account.
- **Age not confirmed:** tap **Request Manual Review**.

## Manual review

- You record a **6 second video** of your face and turn your head once.
- You take a **photo of the front and the back** of an ID document (ID card, passport or driving licence). You may cover the document number.
- Video and photos are stored encrypted and are only seen by the review team to confirm that you are an adult.
- They are **deleted once the decision is made**, at the latest after 7 days. Only the result is kept.
- A review usually takes a few hours. While it is pending you can try the face check again with **Try the Face Check Meanwhile**.
- An approval is valid for 30 days, like the face check.

## Good to know

- On this screen you can still **Sign Out**, **Manage Subscription** if you have a paid plan, or **Delete My Account**.
