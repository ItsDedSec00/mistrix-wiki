# PIN

Your PIN is a number only you know. It locks your private data on your device and confirms it is still you after the age check. Every account has one. There is no way to skip it.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    Setting it: **Onboarding** > **Age Verification & Encryption PIN**, right after your safe word.
    Changing it: **You** > **Privacy & Security** > **Age Verification & Encryption PIN**

## How it works

- The PIN is **6 to 12 digits**. Only digits are accepted.
- It encrypts your private data on your device. Mistrix never sees the PIN itself. What is encrypted and what the server can read is explained once, in [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md).
- You enter it again at least every 7 days, and whenever the app asks for it.

!!! danger "If you forget your PIN"
    Your encrypted data cannot be recovered, not by you and not by us. You must tick "I understand: if I forget my PIN, my encrypted data cannot be recovered" before you can set it.

## Steps

1. Enter your PIN and enter it again under **Confirm PIN**.
2. Tick the box that confirms you understand the warning above.
3. Tap **Set PIN & Continue**. The app encrypts what you entered so far. Onboarding continues with **About You**.

## Good to know

- **Change your PIN** under **You** > **Privacy & Security** > **Age Verification & Encryption PIN**. Enter your **Current PIN**, then your new PIN twice, and tap **Change PIN**. The button reads "Re-encrypting..." while your data is re-encrypted with the new PIN.
- **Forgot your PIN?** On the **Enter PIN** screen, under "Forgot your PIN?", tap **Reset account & delete encrypted data**. Type `DELETE` and tap **Delete data & disable PIN**. This permanently deletes your chat history with every Domina, your fetish map and interests, hard limits, inventory, safe word, routines and open tasks. What your Domina remembers about you and your profile details are kept. You then set a new PIN.
- Write your PIN down somewhere safe, or use a password manager.
