# Mistrix in 5 minutes

Mistrix is a written chat with a Domina or Dom that you choose. Everything is text. These are the points that matter. You do not need to read anything else to start.

!!! info "Where in the app"
    **Dashboard** > **Continue conversation**

## The essentials

- **Pick who you talk to.** You choose a Domina, a Dom or a Trans Domina from the presets. You can change this later. [Choosing a Domina](../chat/choosing-a-domina.md)
- **Just write.** Type in the box at the bottom of the chat. They answer in text, in character. [Messages](../chat/messages.md)
- **Every reply is AI-generated.** A line under the message box says so. It can contain errors. Check anything you are asked to do before you do it.
- **Your safe word stops everything.** Open the **Chat menu** and tap **Safe word**, or type your safe word on its own. [Safe word](../safety/safe-word.md)
- **Hard limits are off limits.** Mark them with the cross on the **Fetish map**. Your Domina is told them every time. A reply can still go wrong, so use the safe word if one is crossed. [Hard limits](../safety/hard-limits.md)
- **You can switch every feature off, at any time.** Chastity, Expose and Findom each have their own switch in **Features**. [Turning things off](../safety/turning-things-off.md)
- **Stop Findom is the safe word for money.** If Findom is on and a demand for Charms appears, **Stop Findom** ends it. [Stop Findom](../safety/stop-findom.md)
- **Your PIN protects your private data.** It encrypts your chats and preferences on your device. If you forget it, that data cannot be recovered. [PIN](pin.md) and [Privacy](../safety/privacy.md)
- **Charms are the in-app currency.** You get some free every day. They pay for pictures and, if you turn Findom on, for tribute. [Charms](../plans/charms.md)

!!! warning "If something is too much"
    Press the safe word, switch the feature off, or take a break. Mistrix does not replace help in a crisis. [Not okay?](../safety/not-okay.md)
